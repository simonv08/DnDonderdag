### History:
Being who I am, you could never be a hero. Traped working a manufactorium with no real connections of passion,
preventing you from effectively pursuing the path of a Hero or even a normal citizen. Even so, a change was bound to
happen but never could i have imagened the kind of change that happend to me. But that's not going to stop me.
You've left your old face behind, taking on a new but familiar persona, becoming something more.

After a examining a strange device at 'work' I died and woke up in a junk cart. Finding some of my possesions I escape
out of the cart. I wander around the town, stealing some clothes, a weapon, and a disguise to hide my undead nature.

_(hidden thoughts, a few months ago)_
I wake up, my body aching like never before. As i regain consciousness I realize that i am not in my sleeping quarters or
even in the manufactorium. I was laying in and partially under a mix of broken cogs, bend springs, malfunctioning
devices, and other junk. Not only that but where ever this was, it was moving.
I carfully sit up and try to remember how i could have ended up here. I remember being given some kind of device that
was covered in runes, traces, and crystals. My task was disassembling it and recording all the component that were used,
broken or probably missing. Vaguest task i had ever been given. Most of the time I'm just tasked with fixing stuff from a
blueprint, but orders are orders.
I remember removing one of the crystals, then a shock, and then ... nothing.
Why, why can't i remember! I am now breathing fast and my heart is raci ... it's not? I try to regain some composure and
hold my hand over my chest and wait.

Nothing.
My heart doesn't beat.
I am dead.
