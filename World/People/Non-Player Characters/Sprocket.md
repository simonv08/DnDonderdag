# Sprocket
_Goblin Rogue, Lawful Evil

**Appears In:** Aurelia

---
_A hyperactive, street-smart smuggler who navigates the sewers, rooftops, and blind spots of Aurelia with ease. Sprocket is responsible for moving illegal magical items past the city guard and sneaking them into the Manufactorium's basement. He is deeply loyal to Silas for getting him out of the slums, though he has a bad habit of skimming minor trinkets for himself._
