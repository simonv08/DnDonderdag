_A devout and unyielding enforcer of the Crown's magical laws. As the Commander of the Arcane Inquisition, Caelum genuinely believes that unrestricted magic will cause a second "Spell-Crackle" and destroy the city. He is not evil, just dangerously inflexible. He highly suspects the Manufactorium is fencing illegal goods, but Silas Vane's paperwork is always too perfect for a legal raid._
