---
_The true arcane genius behind the Manufactorium's success. Sequestered in the heavily warded basement, Lyra is a gruff, soot-covered artisan who can bypass almost any royal magical lock. She lost her official guild license years ago for illegal and dangerous experimentation. She now uses her unmatched skills to spit in the eye of the Crown that exiled her, enjoying the thrill of fixing highly illegal combat magic._
