_Polished, charismatic, and ruthlessly pragmatic. Silas is the public face of the Manufactorium. He schmoozes with Aurelia's nobility and handles the legal side of the business, all while masterminding the massive black-market operation behind closed doors. He fundamentally believes magic should belong to those who can afford it, not just the Crown, and he uses his immense charm to keep the Inquisition looking the other way._
