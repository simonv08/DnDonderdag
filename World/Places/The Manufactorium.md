_From the street, the Manufactorium is a beacon of respectable commerce, smelling of lemon oil and polished brass. But beyond the heavy iron doors meant for employees, the air is choked with the sharp tang of ozone, grease, and sweat. Here, in the grinding lower levels, rows of exhausted, silent workers systematically dismantle jagged, rune-scarred machinery under dim, flickering crystals. It is a suffocating, isolating place where parts are worth more than the people holding them. If you listen closely over the hum of machinery, you can occasionally hear the sharp crack of an arcane misfire, followed by the squeaking wheels of a scrap cart rolling quietly out the back door._

---
The Manufactorium is a two-faced enterprise. On the surface, it is a prestigious, guild-sanctioned repair shop for everyday magical items. Beneath that facade, it is a massive, illicit arcane chop-shop. To handle the most dangerous, unstable illegal magic in the city, the company utilizes a disposable workforce of indebted or unconnected citizens. These lower-level workers are kept isolated and given vague, perilous tasks—like disassembling volatile runic engines or testing untested crystals. When an inevitable and lethal "workplace accident" occurs, the company protects its elite enchanters by silently sweeping the dead workers into junk carts alongside broken cogs and malfunctioning devices, dumping them to avoid the eyes of the Arcane Inquisition.

Originally founded as a modest clockwork and utility magic repair shop, the Manufactorium evolved when Aurelia's black market desperately needed a place to safely fence, repair, and study restricted arcana. As the smuggled contraband grew more dangerous and volatile, the leadership realized their master artificers were at risk of being killed by cursed or trapped items. To protect their most valuable assets, the company implemented the lower "Grinding Floors." They began trapping desperate citizens in dead-end, inescapable contracts, effectively turning normal people into unwitting, disposable bomb-squads for the city's most unpredictable magical devices.
