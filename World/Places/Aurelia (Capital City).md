_Aurelia is a sprawling, walled metropolis of white stone, soaring spires, and everyday magic. Floating crystal lanterns illuminate bustling markets, and griffon-drawn carriages glide over the cobblestones. It is a city so massive and prosperous that some citizens never leave its towering walls. Yet, shadows stretch long beneath the gleaming spires. As an Inquisition patrol marches past—their runic armor glowing with sanctioned authority—the street merchants fall eerily silent. Aurelia is a beautiful, gilded cage where utility magic is a given, but true arcane power is hoarded by the Crown and fiercely fought over in the dark._

---
Aurelia is the undisputed center of the world's economy and a massive urban sandbox. Because the campaign takes place entirely within its walls, the city is divided into highly distinct districts—from the wealthy, heavily policed Sun-Touched Ward to the unmonitored, subterranean Weave-Blind Alleys. The city operates on a tense balance of power between the ruling Crown (enforced by the Arcane Inquisition) and the thriving magical underworld (anchored by [[the Manufactorium]]). Actions here have permanent consequences; players will need to carefully manage their alliances, build local reputations, and avoid drawing too much "heat" from the guards.

Centuries ago, Aurelia was founded directly atop a massive convergence of arcane ley lines, drawing scholars and mages from across the realm. However, a catastrophic event known as "The Spell-Crackle"—caused by unregulated, experimental wizardry—nearly tore the city apart. In the aftermath, the Crown walled off the city and formed the Arcane Inquisition to strictly license all combat magic and ancient artifacts. This brought generations of economic prosperity but inadvertently birthed a massive, highly organized black market to supply the city's insatiable demand for restricted magic.
