# Potion of Speed

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Haste]] spell for 1 minute (no [[Statuss#Concentration|Concentration]] required) without suffering the wave of lethargy that typically occurs when the effect ends.

This potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own.
