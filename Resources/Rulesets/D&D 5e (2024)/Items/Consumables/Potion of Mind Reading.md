# Potion of Mind Reading

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Detect Thoughts]] spell (save 13) for 10 minutes (no [[Statuss#Concentration|Concentration]] required).

This potion's dense, purple liquid has an ovoid cloud of pink floating in it.
