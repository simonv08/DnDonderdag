# Sled Dog

A sled dog (use the [[Wolf]] stat block in appendix A of the Monster Manual) costs 50 gp and can pull 360 pounds.

Sled dogs must take a short rest after pulling a sled for 1 hour; otherwise, they gain one level of [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
