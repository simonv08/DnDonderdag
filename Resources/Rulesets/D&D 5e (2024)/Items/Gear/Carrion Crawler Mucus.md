# Carrion Crawler Mucus

A creature subjected to Carrion Crawler Mucus must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The creature also has the [[Conditions#Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition while [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
