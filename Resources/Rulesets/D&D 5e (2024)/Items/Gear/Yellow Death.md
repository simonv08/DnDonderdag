# Yellow Death

A creature subjected to Yellow Death makes a 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 14 (4d6) Poison damage and has the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.
