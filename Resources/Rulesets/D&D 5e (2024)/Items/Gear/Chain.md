# Chain

As a [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Conditions#Grappled|Grappled]], [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a 13 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check. If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful 18 Dexterity ([[Skills#Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a successful 20 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check as an action.
