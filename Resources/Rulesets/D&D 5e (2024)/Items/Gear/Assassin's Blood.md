# Assassin's Blood

A creature subjected to Assassin's Blood makes a 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 6 (1d12) Poison damage and has the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.
