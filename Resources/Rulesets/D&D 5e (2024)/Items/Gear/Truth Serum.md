# Truth Serum

A creature subjected to Truth Serum must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour. The [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] creature can't knowingly communicate a lie.
