# Acid

When you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a vial of Acid. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (8 plus your Dexterity modifier and ) or take 2d6 Acid damage.
