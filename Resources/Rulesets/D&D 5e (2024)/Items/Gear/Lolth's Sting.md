# Lolth's Sting

A creature subjected to Lolth's Sting must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour. If the creature fails the save by 5 or more, the creature also has the [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition while [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
