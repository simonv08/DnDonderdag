# Prespra

A creature subjected to Prespra must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or take 3 (1d6) Poison damage and have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition. The creature also has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition while [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The creature repeats the save at the start of each of its turns, taking 3 (1d6) Poison damage on a failed save. On a successful save, the effect ends for the creature.
