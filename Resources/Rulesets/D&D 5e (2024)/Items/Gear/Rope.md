# Rope

As a [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can tie a knot with Rope if you succeed on a 10 Dexterity ([[Skills#Sleight of Hand|Sleight Of Hand]]) check. The Rope can be burst with a successful 20 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check.

You can bind an unwilling creature with the Rope only if the creature has the [[Conditions#Grappled|Grappled]], [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition. If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition until it escapes. Escaping the Rope requires the creature to make a successful 15 Dexterity ([[Skills#Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check as an action.
