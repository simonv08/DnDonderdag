# Healer's Kit

A Healer's Kit has ten uses. As a [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can expend one of its uses to stabilize an [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]] creature that has 0  without needing to make a Wisdom ([[Skills#Medicine|Medicine]]) check.
