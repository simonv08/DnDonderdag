# Eyescratch

A creature that comes into contact with this poison must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour and [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. A [[Lesser Restoration]] spell or similar magic ends the effect.
