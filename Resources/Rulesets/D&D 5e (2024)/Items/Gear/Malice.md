# Malice

A creature subjected to Malice must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour. The creature also has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition while [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way.
