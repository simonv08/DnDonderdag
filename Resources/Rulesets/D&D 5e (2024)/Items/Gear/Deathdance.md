# Deathdance

A creature subjected to Deathdance must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. Humanoids automatically succeed on this save. The creature also has the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition while [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way, as it thrashes uncontrollably.
