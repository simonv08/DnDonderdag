# Torch

A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting  in a 20-foot radius and  for an additional 20 feet. When you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action, you can attack with the Torch, using it as a Simple Melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.
