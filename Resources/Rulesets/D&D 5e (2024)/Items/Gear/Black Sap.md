# Black Sap

This tarry substance harvested from the dark boughs of the death's head willow is a powerful intoxicant. It can be smoked as a concentrate or injected directly into the bloodstream. A creature subjected to a dose of black sap cannot be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] for 1d6 hours.

For each dose of black sap consumed, a creature must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 2d4 hours—an effect that is cumulative with multiple doses.
