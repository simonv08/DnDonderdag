# Ball Bearings

As a [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can spill Ball Bearings from their pouch. They spread to cover a level, 10-foot-square area within 10 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a 10 Dexterity saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Ball Bearings.
