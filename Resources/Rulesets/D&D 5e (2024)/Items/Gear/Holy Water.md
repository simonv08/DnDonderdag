# Holy Water

When you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of Holy Water. Target one creature you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (8 plus your Dexterity modifier and ) or take 2d8 Radiant damage if it is a Fiend or an Undead.
