# Monster Camouflage

A suit of Monster Camouflage looks like a Beast or Monstrosity, such as an [[Owlbear]]. To discern that you're disguised, a creature must take the [[Actions#Study|Study]] action to inspect your appearance and succeed on a DC 10 Intelligence ([[Skills#Investigation|Investigation]] or [[Skills#Nature|Nature]]) check. The creature has  on this check if it is within 30 feet of you and automatically succeeds on this check if you do anything the monster you're disguised as couldn't do.
