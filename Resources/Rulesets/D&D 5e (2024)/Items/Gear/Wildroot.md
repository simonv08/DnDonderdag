# Wildroot

Introducing the juice of a wildroot into a [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature's bloodstream (for example, by rubbing it on an open wound) rids the creature of the [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] condition. Once used in this way, a wildroot loses this property.
