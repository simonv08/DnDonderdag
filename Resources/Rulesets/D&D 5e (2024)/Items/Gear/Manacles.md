# Manacles

As a [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can use Manacles to bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Conditions#Grappled|Grappled]], [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a 13 Dexterity ([[Skills#Sleight of Hand|Sleight Of Hand]]) check. While bound, a creature has  on attack rolls, and the creature is [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] if the Manacles are attached to a chain or hook that is fixed in place. Escaping the Manacles requires a successful 20 Dexterity ([[Skills#Sleight of Hand|Sleight Of Hand]]) check as an action. Bursting them requires a successful 25 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check as an action.

Each set of Manacles comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use [[Thieves' Tools]] to pick the Manacles' lock with a successful 15 Dexterity ([[Skills#Sleight of Hand|Sleight Of Hand]]) check.
