# Midnight Tears

A creature that ingests Midnight Tears suffers no effect until the stroke of midnight. Any effect that ends the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition neutralizes this poison. If the poison hasn't been neutralized before midnight, the creature makes a 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 31 (9d6) Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
