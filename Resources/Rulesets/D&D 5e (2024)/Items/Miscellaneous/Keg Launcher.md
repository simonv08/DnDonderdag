# Keg Launcher

A back-mounted, wooden catapult flings small kegs of toxic gas. Loading a Keg Launcher requires the [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action, and aiming it requires another [[Actions#Utilize|Utilize]] action. Then a crew member can take the Toxic Keg action.
