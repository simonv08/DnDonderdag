# Concussion Grenade

[[Grenade]]

This grenade explodes in a concussive blast that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere. Each creature in that area must make a 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking (6d6) force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
