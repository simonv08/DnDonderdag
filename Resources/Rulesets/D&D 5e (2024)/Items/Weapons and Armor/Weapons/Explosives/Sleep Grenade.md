# Sleep Grenade

[[Grenade]]

This grenade releases a cloud of soporific mist that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere, then dissipates. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for 1 hour. The condition ends on a creature early if the creature takes damage or if another creature uses an action to shake it awake.
