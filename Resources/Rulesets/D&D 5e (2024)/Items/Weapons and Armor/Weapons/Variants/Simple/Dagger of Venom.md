# Dagger of Venom

You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can take a  to magically coat the blade with poison. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or take 2d10 Poison damage and have the [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The weapon can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
