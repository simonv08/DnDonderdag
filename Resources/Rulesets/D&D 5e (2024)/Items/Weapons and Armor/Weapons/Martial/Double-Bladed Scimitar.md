# Double-Bladed Scimitar

### *Martial Weapon*
**Category**: Martial
**Damage**: 2d4
**Damage Type**: Slashing
**Range**: —
**Reload**: —
**Properties**: Special, Two-Handed
#### Special
If you attack with a double-bladed scimitar as part of the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action on your turn, you can use a bonus action immediately after to make a melee attack with it. This attack deals 1d4 slashing damage on a hit, instead of 2d4.
