# Spiked Shield

Crude metal spikes adorn this magic Shield. It has the following properties that you can use while holding it.
#### Shield Bash
When you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the Shield against a target within 5 feet of yourself. Apply your  and Strength modifier to the attack roll. On a hit, the Shield deals Piercing damage equal to 2d6 plus your Strength modifier.
#### Spike Salvo
As a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action, you can cause spikes to erupt from the Shield in a 30-foot . Each creature in the  makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Piercing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
