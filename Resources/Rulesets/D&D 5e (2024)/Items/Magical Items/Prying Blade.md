# Prying Blade

A hooked blade useful on board a ship or as a cutting tool in the wilderness, this [[+1 Shortsword]] grants its wielder advantage on Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) checks to climb or to escape while [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]].
