# Euryale Card

As an action, you can brandish this card at one creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself and attempt to turn it to stone. The target must succeed on a 17 Constitution saving throw, or it begins to turn to stone and has the [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] condition. The affected target must repeat the saving throw at the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the effect ends on the target. On a failed save, the target has the [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] condition (instead of the [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] condition) until it is removed by the [[Greater Restoration]] spell or similar magic. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
