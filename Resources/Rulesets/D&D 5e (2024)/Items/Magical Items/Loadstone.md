# Loadstone

This stone is a large gem worth 150 gp.
#### Curse
The stone is cursed, but its magical nature is hidden; [[Detect Magic]] doesn't detect it. An [[Identify]] spell reveals the stone's true nature. If you use the [[Actions#Dash|Dash]] or [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] action while the stone is on your person, its curse activates. Until the curse is broken with [[Remove Curse]] or similar magic, your speed is reduced by 5 feet, and your maximum load and maximum lift capacities are halved. You also become unwilling to part with the stone.
