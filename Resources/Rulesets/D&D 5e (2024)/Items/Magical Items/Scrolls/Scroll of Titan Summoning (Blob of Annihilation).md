# Scroll of Titan Summoning (Blob of Annihilation)

When you take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to read this scroll, a [[Blob Of Annihilation]] appears in an unoccupied space on the ground or in water that you can see within 1 mile of yourself.

The [[Blob Of Annihilation]] is  toward all other creatures and disappears when it drops to 0 . If the [[Blob Of Annihilation]] is summoned into a space that isn't large enough to contain it, the summoning fails, and the scroll is wasted.
