# Scroll of Titan Summoning (Kraken)

When you take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to read this scroll, a [[Kraken]] appears in an unoccupied space in water that you can see within 1 mile of yourself. A [[Kraken]] requires a body of water large enough to contain it, or the summoning fails and the scroll is wasted

The [[Kraken]] is  toward all other creatures and disappears when it drops to 0 . If the [[Kraken]] is summoned into a space that isn't large enough to contain it, the summoning fails, and the scroll is wasted.
