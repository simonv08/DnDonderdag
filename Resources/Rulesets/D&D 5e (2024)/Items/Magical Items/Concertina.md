# Concertina

A concertina is a less charming kind of accordion.

In addition to knocking out a killer polka, a creature playing the concertina can use an action to cause an area of mist or water up to 30 feet in diameter and 6 inches deep to freeze solid.

Once used, this feature of the concertina can't be used again until the next dawn.

Any creatures touching the floor must succeed on a 20 Dexterity saving throw or be [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by the ice. As an action, a [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] creature can attempt to free itself or another creature within its reach from the ice, doing so with a successful 20 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check. A character freed from the ice is no longer [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by it. The ice melts in an hour.
