# Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

While touching this 5-pound stone to the ground, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to summon an [[Earth Elemental]]. The elemental appears in an unoccupied space you choose within 30 feet of yourself, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your  count. The elemental disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a . The stone can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
