# Blast Scepter

The Blast Scepter can be used as an arcane focus.

Whoever is attuned to the Blast Scepter gains resistance to fire and lightning damage and can, as an action, use it to cast [[Thunderwave]] as a 4th-level spell (save 16) without expending a spell slot.
