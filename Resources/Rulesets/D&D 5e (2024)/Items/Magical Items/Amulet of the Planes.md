# Amulet of the Planes

While wearing this amulet, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to name a location that you are familiar with on another plane of existence. Then make a 15 Intelligence ([[Skills#Arcana|Arcana]]) check. On a successful check, you cast [[Plane Shift]]. On a failed check, you and each creature and object within 15 feet of you travel to a random destination determined by rolling 1d100 and consulting the following table.
