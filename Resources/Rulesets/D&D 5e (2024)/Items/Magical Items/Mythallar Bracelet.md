# Mythallar Bracelet

Three small, crystal beads made from a decommissioned mythallar are strung on this leather bracelet. As a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action, you can pluck one bead from the bracelet to gain  on Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) checks for 1 minute.

A bead disappears immediately after it's plucked. Once all three beads have been plucked, the bracelet loses its magic.
