# Broom of Flying

This wooden broom functions like a mundane broom until you stand astride it and take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to make it hover beneath you, at which time it can be ridden in the air. It has a  of 50 feet. It can carry up to 400 pounds, but its  becomes 30 feet while carrying over 200 pounds. The broom stops hovering when you land or when you're no longer riding it.

As a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action, you can send the broom to travel alone to a destination within 1 mile of you if you name the location and are familiar with it. The broom comes back to you when you take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action and use a command word if the broom is still within 1 mile of you.
