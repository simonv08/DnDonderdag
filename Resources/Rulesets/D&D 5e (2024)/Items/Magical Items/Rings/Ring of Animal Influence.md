# Ring of Animal Influence

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save 13) from it:

- [[Animal Friendship]]
- [[Fear]] (affects Beasts only)
- [[Speak With Animals]]
