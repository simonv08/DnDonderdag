# Horn of Valhalla, Silver

You can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to blow this horn. In response, warrior spirits from the plane of Ysgard appear in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet of you. Each spirit uses the [[Berserker]] stat block and returns to Ysgard after 1 hour or when it drops to 0 . The spirits look like living, breathing warriors, and they have  to the [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]] conditions. Once you use the horn, it can't be used again until 7 days have passed.

A silver horn summons 2 [[Berserker]]. They are  to you and your allies and follow your commands.
