# Arcane Battery

This smooth, oval stone is etched with faintly glowing, magical symbols. While holding the battery, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to touch the battery to one magic item. If that magic item normally regains expended charges daily, it immediately regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges, and the battery loses its magic.
