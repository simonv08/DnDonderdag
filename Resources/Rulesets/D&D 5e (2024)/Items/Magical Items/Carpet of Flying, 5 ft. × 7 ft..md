# Carpet of Flying, 5 ft. × 7 ft.

You can make this carpet hover and fly by taking a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action and using the carpet's command word. It moves according to your directions if you are within 30 feet of it.

A 5 ft. × 7 ft. carpet can carry up to 600 lb. at a fly speed of 40 feet. A carpet can carry up to twice the weight shown on the table, but its  is halved if it carries more than its normal capacity.
