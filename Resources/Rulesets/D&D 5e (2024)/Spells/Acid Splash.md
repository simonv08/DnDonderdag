# Acid Splash
### *Evocation Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create an acidic bubble at a point within range, where it explodes in a 5-foot-radius . Each creature in that  must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Acid damage.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
