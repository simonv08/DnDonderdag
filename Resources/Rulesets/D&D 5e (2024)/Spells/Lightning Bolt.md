# Lightning Bolt
### *3rd-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fur and a crystal rod)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A stroke of lightning forming a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide  blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the  makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
