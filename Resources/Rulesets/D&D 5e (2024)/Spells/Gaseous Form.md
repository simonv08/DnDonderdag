# Gaseous Form
### *3rd-level Transmutation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of gauze)
**Duration:** 1 hour (concentration)

A willing creature you touch shape-shifts, along with everything it's wearing and carrying, into a misty cloud for the duration. The spell ends on the target if it drops to 0  or if it takes a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to end the spell on itself.

While in this form, the target's only method of movement is a  of 10 feet, and it can hover. The target can enter and occupy the space of another creature. The target has  to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage; it has  to the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition; and it has  on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. The target can pass through narrow openings, but it treats liquids as though they were solid surfaces.

The target can't talk or manipulate objects, and any objects it was carrying or holding can't be dropped, used, or otherwise interacted with. Finally, the target can't attack or cast spells.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3.
