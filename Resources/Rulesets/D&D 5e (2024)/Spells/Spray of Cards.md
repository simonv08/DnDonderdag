# Spray of Cards
### *2nd-level Conjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a deck of cards)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You spray a 15-foot cone of spectral cards. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d10 force damage and has the [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

##### At Higher Levels
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.
