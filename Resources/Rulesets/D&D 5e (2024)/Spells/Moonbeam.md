# Moonbeam
### *2nd-level Evocation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a moonseed leaf)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

A silvery beam of pale light shines down in a 5-foot-radius, 40-foot-high  centered on a point within range. Until the spell ends,  fills the , and you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action on later turns to move the  up to 60 feet.

When the  appears, each creature in it makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d10 Radiant damage, and if the creature is shape-shifted (as a result of the [[Polymorph]] spell, for example), it reverts to its true form and can't shape-shift until it leaves the . On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. A creature also makes this save when the spell's area moves into its space and when it enters the spell's area or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 2.
