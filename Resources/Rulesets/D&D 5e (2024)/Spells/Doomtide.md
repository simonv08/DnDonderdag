# Doomtide
### *4th-level Conjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (soot and a dried eel)
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You create a 20-foot-radius  of inky fog within range. The fog is magical  and lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by the [[Gust Of Wind]] spell) disperses it, ending the spell.

Each creature in the  when it appears makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d6 Psychic damage and subtracts 1d6 from its saving throws until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. A creature also makes this save when the  moves into its space, when it enters the , or when it ends its turn inside the . A creature makes this save only once per turn.

The  moves 10 feet away from you at the start of each of your turns.

##### Casting as a Circle Spell
Casting this as a Circle spell requires a minimum of five secondary casters. In addition to the spell's usual components, you must provide a special component (a string of three black pearls from Pandemonium), which the spell consumes. The spell's range increases to 1 mile, and its duration increases to until dispelled (no [[Statuss#Concentration|Concentration]] required). The spell ends early if any caster who participated in this casting contributes to another casting of [[Doomtide]] as a Circle spell.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 3+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.
