# Healing Word
### *1st-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature of your choice that you can see within range regains  equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The healing increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
