# Yolande's Regal Presence
### *5th-level Enchantment Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a miniature tiara)
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You surround yourself with unearthly majesty in a 10-foot . Whenever the  enters the space of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters the  or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 4d6 Psychic damage and has the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition, and you can push it up to 10 feet away. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
