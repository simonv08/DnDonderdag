# Shatter
### *2nd-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a chip of mica)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A loud noise erupts from a point of your choice within range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius  centered there makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 3d8 Thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A Construct has  on the save.

A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.
