# Prismatic Spray
### *7th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Eight rays of light flash from you in a 60-foot . Each creature in the  makes a Dexterity saving throw. For each target, roll 1d8 to determine which color ray affects it, consulting the Prismatic Rays table.

##### Prismatic Rays
| 1d8 | Ray |
| :-- | :-- |
| 1 |   12d6 Fire damage.  Half as much damage. |
| 2 |   12d6 Acid damage.  Half as much damage. |
| 3 |   12d6 Lightning damage.  Half as much damage. |
| 4 |   12d6 Poison damage.  Half as much damage. |
| 5 |   12d6 Cold damage.  Half as much damage. |
| 6 |   The target has the [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition and makes a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If it successfully saves three times, the condition ends. If it fails three times, it has the [[Conditions#Petrified|Petrified]] condition until it is freed by an effect like the [[Greater Restoration]] spell. The successes and failures needn't be consecutive; keep track of both until the target collects three of a kind. |
| 7 |   The target has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition and makes a Wisdom saving throw at the start of your next turn. On a successful save, the condition ends. On a failed save, the condition ends, and the creature teleports to another plane of existence (DM's choice). |
| 8 |  The target is struck by two rays. Roll twice, rerolling any 8. |
