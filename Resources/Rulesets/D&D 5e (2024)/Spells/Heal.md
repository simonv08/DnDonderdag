# Heal
### *6th-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. Positive energy washes through the target, restoring 70 . This spell also ends the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]], and [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions on the target.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The healing increases by 10 for each spell slot level above 6.
