# Cure Wounds
### *1st-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A creature you touch regains a number of  equal to 2d8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The healing increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
