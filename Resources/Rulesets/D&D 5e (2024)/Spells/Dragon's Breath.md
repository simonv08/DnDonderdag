# Dragon's Breath
### *2nd-level Transmutation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a hot pepper)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You touch one willing creature, and choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until the spell ends, the target can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to exhale a 15-foot . Each creature in that area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
