# Enhance Ability
### *2nd-level Transmutation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (fur or a feather)
**Duration:** 1 hour (concentration)

You touch a creature and choose Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. For the duration, the target has  on ability checks using the chosen ability.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2. You can choose a different ability for each target.
