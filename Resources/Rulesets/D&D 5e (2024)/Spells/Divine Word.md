# Divine Word
### *7th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V
**Saving Throw:** Charisma
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You utter a word imbued with power from the Upper Planes. Each creature of your choice in range makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, a target that has 50  or fewer suffers an effect based on its current , as shown in the Divine Word Effects table. Regardless of its , a Celestial, an Elemental, a Fey, or a Fiend target that fails its save is forced back to its plane of origin (if it isn't there already) and can't return to the current plane for 24 hours by any means short of a [[Wish]] spell.

##### Divine Word Effects
| Hit Points | Effect |
| :-- | :-- |
| 0-20 | The target dies. |
| 21-30 | The target has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]], and [[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]] conditions for 1 hour. |
| 31-40 | The target has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] and [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]] conditions for 10 minutes. |
| 41-50 | The target has the [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]] condition for 1 minute. |
