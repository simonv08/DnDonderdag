# Pass without Trace
### *2nd-level Abjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (ashes from burned mistletoe)
**Duration:** 1 hour (concentration)

You radiate a concealing aura in a 30-foot  for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks and leave no tracks.
