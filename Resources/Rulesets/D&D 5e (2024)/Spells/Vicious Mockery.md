# Vicious Mockery
### *Enchantment Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a string of insults laced with subtle enchantments at one creature you can see or hear within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d6 Psychic damage and have  on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
