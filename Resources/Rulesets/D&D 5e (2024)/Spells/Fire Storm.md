# Fire Storm
### *7th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 150 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A storm of fire appears within range. The area of the storm consists of up to ten 10-foot Cubes, which you arrange as you like. Each  must be contiguous with at least one other . Each creature in the area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 7d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
