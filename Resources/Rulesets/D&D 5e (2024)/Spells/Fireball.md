# Fireball
### *3rd-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 150 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius  centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start .

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
