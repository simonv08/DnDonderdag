# Starry Wisp
### *Evocation Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a mote of light at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Radiant damage, and until the end of your next turn, it emits  in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from the [[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]] condition.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
