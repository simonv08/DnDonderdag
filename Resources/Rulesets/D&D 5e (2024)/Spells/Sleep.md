# Sleep
### *1st-level Enchantment Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sand or rose petals)
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

Each creature of your choice in a 5-foot-radius  centered on a point within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn, at which point it must repeat the save. If the target fails the second save, the target has the [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for the duration. The spell ends on a target if it takes damage or someone within 5 feet of it takes an action to shake it out of the spell's effect.

Creatures that don't sleep, such as elves, or that have  to the [[Conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] condition automatically succeed on saves against this spell.
