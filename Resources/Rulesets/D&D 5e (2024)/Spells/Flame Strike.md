# Flame Strike
### *5th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sulfur)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A vertical column of brilliant fire roars down from above. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high  centered on a point within range makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d6 Fire damage and 5d6 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The Fire damage and the Radiant damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.
