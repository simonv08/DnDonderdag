# Power Word Kill
### *9th-level Enchantment Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You compel one creature you can see within range to die. If the target has 100  or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes 12d12 Psychic damage.
