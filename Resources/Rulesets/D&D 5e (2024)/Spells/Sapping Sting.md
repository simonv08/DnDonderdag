# Sapping Sting
### *Necromancy Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You sap the vitality of one creature you can see in range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d4 necrotic damage and fall [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

This spell's damage increases by 1d4 when you reach 5th level (2d4), 11th level (3d4), and 17th level (4d4).
