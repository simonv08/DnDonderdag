# Alarm
### *1st-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a bell and silver wire)
**Duration:** 8 hour

You set an alarm against intrusion. Choose a door, a window, or an area within range that is no larger than a 20-foot . Until the spell ends, an alarm alerts you whenever a creature touches or enters the warded area. When you cast the spell, you can designate creatures that won't set off the alarm.
