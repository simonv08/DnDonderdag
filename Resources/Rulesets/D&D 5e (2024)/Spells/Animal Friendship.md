# Animal Friendship
### *1st-level Enchantment Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 24 hour

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spell ends.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
