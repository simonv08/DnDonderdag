# Regenerate
### *7th-level Transmutation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a prayer wheel)
**Duration:** 1 hour

A creature you touch regains 4d8 + 15 . For the duration, the target regains 1  at the start of each of its turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.
