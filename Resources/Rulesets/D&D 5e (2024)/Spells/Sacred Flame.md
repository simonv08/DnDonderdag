# Sacred Flame
### *Evocation Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 Radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from  or Three-Quarters  for this save.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
