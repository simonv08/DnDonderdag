# Ensnaring Strike
### *1st-level Conjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Saving Throw:** Strength
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

As you hit the target, grasping vines appear on it, and it makes a Strength saving throw. A Large or larger creature has  on this save. On a failed save, the target has the [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. On a successful save, the vines shrivel away, and the spell ends.

While [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]], the target takes 1d6 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check against your spell save DC. On a success, the spell ends.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
