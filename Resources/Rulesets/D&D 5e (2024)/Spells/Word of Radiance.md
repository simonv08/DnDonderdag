# Word of Radiance
### *Evocation Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, M (a sunburst token)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Burning radiance erupts from you in a 5-foot . Each creature of your choice that you can see in it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 Radiant damage.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
