# Cloud of Daggers
### *2nd-level Conjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a sliver of glass)
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You conjure spinning daggers in a 5-foot  centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area takes 4d4 Slashing damage. A creature also takes this damage if it enters the  or ends its turn there or if the  moves into its space. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

On your later turns, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to teleport the  up to 30 feet.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 2.
