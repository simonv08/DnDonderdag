# Ice Storm
### *4th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 300 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a mitten)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Hail falls in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high  centered on a point within range. Each creature in the  makes a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 2d10 Bludgeoning damage and 4d6 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Hailstones turn ground in the  into  until the end of your next turn.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The Bludgeoning damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 4.
