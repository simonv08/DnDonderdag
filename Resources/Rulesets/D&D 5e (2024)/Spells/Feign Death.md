# Feign Death
### *3rd-level Necromancy Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of graveyard dirt)
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature and put it into a cataleptic state that is indistinguishable from death.

For the duration, the target appears dead to outward inspection and to spells used to determine the target's status. The target has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] and [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] conditions, and its  is 0.
