# Wardaway
### *1st-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a miniature clay hand)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a disorienting magical force toward one creature within range. The target makes a Constitution saving throw; Constructs and Undead automatically succeed on this save.

On a failed save, the target takes 2d4 Force damage, its  is halved until the start of your next turn, and on its next turn, it can take only an action or a  (but not both). On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 2d4 for every spell slot level above 1.
