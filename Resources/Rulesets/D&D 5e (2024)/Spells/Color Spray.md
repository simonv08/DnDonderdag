# Color Spray
### *1st-level Illusion Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colorful sand)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot  originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.
