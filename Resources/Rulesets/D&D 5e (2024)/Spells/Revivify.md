# Revivify
### *3rd-level Necromancy Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a diamond worth 300+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature revives with 1 . This spell can't revive a creature that has died of old age, nor does it restore any missing body parts.
