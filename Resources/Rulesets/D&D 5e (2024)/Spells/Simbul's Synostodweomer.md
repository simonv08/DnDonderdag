# Simbul's Synostodweomer
### *7th-level Transmutation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 hour

You imbue one creature you touch with magical healing energy for the duration. Whenever the target casts a spell using a spell slot, the target can immediately roll a number of unexpended  equal to the spell slot's level and regain  equal to the roll's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier; those dice are then expended.
