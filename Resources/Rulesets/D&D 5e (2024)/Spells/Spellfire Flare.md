# Spellfire Flare
### *1st-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of brilliant fire. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range; a target gains no benefit from  or  for this attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d10 Radiant damage.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
You create an additional blast for each spell slot level above 1. You can direct the blasts at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each blast.
