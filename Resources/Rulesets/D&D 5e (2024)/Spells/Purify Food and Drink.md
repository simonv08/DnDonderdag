# Purify Food and Drink
### *1st-level Transmutation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 10 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical food and drink in a 5-foot-radius  centered on a point within range.
