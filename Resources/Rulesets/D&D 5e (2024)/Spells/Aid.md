# Aid
### *2nd-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a strip of white cloth)
**Duration:** 8 hour

Choose up to three creatures within range. Each target's  maximum and current  increase by 5 for the duration.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
Each target's  increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 2.
