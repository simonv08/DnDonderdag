# Synaptic Static
### *5th-level Enchantment Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Intelligence, Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You cause psychic energy to erupt at a point within range. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius  centered on that point makes an Intelligence saving throw, taking 8d6 Psychic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

On a failed save, a target also has muddled thoughts for 1 minute. During that time, it subtracts 1d6 from all its attack rolls and ability checks, as well as any Constitution saving throws to maintain [[Statuss#Concentration|Concentration]]. The target makes an Intelligence saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
