# Destructive Wave
### *5th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Destructive energy ripples outward from you in a 30-foot . Each creature you choose in the  makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 5d6 Thunder damage and 5d6 Radiant or Necrotic damage (your choice) and has the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.
