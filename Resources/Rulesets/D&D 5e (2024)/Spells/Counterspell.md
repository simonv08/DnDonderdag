# Counterspell
### *3rd-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 reaction
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** S
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell. The creature makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, , or  used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot, the slot isn't expended.
