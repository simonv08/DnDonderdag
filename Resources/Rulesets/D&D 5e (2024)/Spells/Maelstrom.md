# Maelstrom
### *5th-level Evocation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (paper or leaf in the shape of a funnel)
**Saving Throw:** Strength
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

A swirling mass of 5-foot-deep water appears in a 30-foot radius centered on a point you can see within range. The point must be on the ground or in a body of water. Until the spell ends, that area is [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]], and any creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take 6d6 bludgeoning damage and be pulled 10 feet toward the center.
