# Mass Healing Word
### *3rd-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to six creatures of your choice that you can see within range regain  equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The healing increases by 1d4 for each spell slot level above 3.
