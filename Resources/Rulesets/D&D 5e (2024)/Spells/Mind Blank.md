# Mind Blank
### *8th-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 24 hour

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has  to Psychic damage and the [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]] condition. The target is also unaffected by anything that would sense its emotions or alignment, read its thoughts, or magically detect its location, and no spell—not even [[Wish]]—can gather information about the target, observe it remotely, or control its mind.
