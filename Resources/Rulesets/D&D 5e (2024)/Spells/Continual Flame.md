# Continual Flame
### *2nd-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (ruby dust worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Permanent

A flame springs from an object that you touch. The effect casts  in a 20-foot radius and  for an additional 20 feet. It looks like a regular flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched.
