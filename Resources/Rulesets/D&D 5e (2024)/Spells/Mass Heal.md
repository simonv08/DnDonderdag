# Mass Heal
### *9th-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A flood of healing energy flows from you into creatures around you. You restore up to 700 , divided as you choose among any number of creatures that you can see within range. Creatures healed by this spell also have the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]], and [[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions removed from them.
