# Sunbeam
### *6th-level Evocation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a magnifying glass)
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You launch a sunbeam in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long . Each creature in the  makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Radiant damage and has the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Until the spell ends, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to create a new  of radiance.

For the duration, a mote of brilliant radiance shines above you. It sheds  in a 30-foot radius and  for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight.
