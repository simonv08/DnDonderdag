# Hypnotic Pattern
### *3rd-level Illusion Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** S, M (a pinch of confetti)
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 30-foot  within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While [[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]], the creature has the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition and a  of 0.

The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses an action to shake the creature out of its stupor.
