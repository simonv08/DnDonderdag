# Conjure Barrage
### *3rd-level Conjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V, S, M (a Melee or Ranged weapon worth at least 1 CP)
**Saving Throw:** Dexterity
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You brandish the weapon used to cast the spell and conjure similar spectral weapons (or ammunition appropriate to the weapon) that launch forward and then disappear. Each creature of your choice that you can see in a 60-foot  makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot
The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
