# Zone of Truth
### *2nd-level Enchantment Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Charisma
**Duration:** 10 minute

You create a magical zone that guards against deception in a 15-foot-radius  centered on a point within range. Until the spell ends, a creature that enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, a creature can't speak a deliberate lie while in the radius. You know whether a creature succeeds or fails on this save.

An affected creature is aware of the spell and can avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive yet must be truthful.
