# Thunderclap
### *Evocation Cantrip*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** S
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Each creature in a 5-foot  originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 Thunder damage. The spell's thunderous sound can be heard up to 100 feet away.

##### Cantrip Upgrade
The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
