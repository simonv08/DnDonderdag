# Aura of Vitality
### *3rd-level Abjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V
**Duration:** 1 minute (concentration)

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot  for the duration. When you create the aura and at the start of each of your turns while it persists, you can restore 2d6  to one creature in it.
