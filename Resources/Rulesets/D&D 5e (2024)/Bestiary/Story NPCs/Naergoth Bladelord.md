# Naergoth Bladelord

Medium Undead, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 135
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Wisdom
**Skills**: Perception +6, Stealth +5
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 11

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 12 | 16 | 12 | 14 | 16 |

### Traits
***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, Naergoth has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Naergoth makes three attacks, either with his longsword or longbow. He can use Life Drain in place of one longsword attack.

***Life Drain.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 20 (5d6 + 3) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

***Longsword.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (1d8 + 5) slashing damage, or 10 (1d10 + 5) if used with two hands, plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

***Longbow.*** 5 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.
