### Traits
***Siege Monster.*** Zalto deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Special Equipment.*** Zalto wears a [[Ring Of Lightning Resistance]].

***Tackle.*** When Zalto enters any enemy's space for the first time on a turn, the enemy must succeed on a 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Zalto makes two maul attacks.

***Maul.*** 11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 28 (6d6 + 7) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** 11 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. 29 (4d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
