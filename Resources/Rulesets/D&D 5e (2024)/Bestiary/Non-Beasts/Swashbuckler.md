# Swashbuckler

Medium Humanoid (Any race), NX Chaotic Good NY Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 66
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Acrobatics +8, Athletics +5, Persuasion +6
**Languages**: Any one language (usually Common)
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 18 | 12 | 14 | 11 | 15 |

### Traits
***Lightfooted.*** The swashbuckler can take the [[Actions#Dash|Dash]] or [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

***Suave Defense.*** While the swashbuckler is wearing light or no armor and wielding no shield, its AC includes its Charisma modifier.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The swashbuckler makes three attacks: one with a dagger and two with its rapier.

***Dagger.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing damage.

***Rapier.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
