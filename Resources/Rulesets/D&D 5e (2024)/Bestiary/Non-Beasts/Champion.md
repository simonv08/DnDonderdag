# Champion

Medium Humanoid (Any race), A

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 143
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Constitution
**Skills**: Athletics +9, Intimidation +5, Perception +6
**Languages**: Any one language (usually Common)
**Challenge**: 9

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Indomitable (2/Day).*** The champion rerolls a failed saving throw.

***Second Wind (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** As a bonus action, the champion can regain 20 hit points.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The champion makes three attacks with its greatsword or its shortbow.

***Greatsword.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage, plus 7 (2d6) slashing damage if the champion has more than half of its total hit points remaining.

***Shortbow.*** 6 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, plus 7 (2d6) piercing damage if the champion has more than half of its total hit points remaining.
