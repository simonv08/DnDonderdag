# Knight

Medium Humanoid (Any race), A

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 52
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Wisdom
**Languages**: Any one language (usually Common)
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 11 | 14 | 11 | 11 | 15 |

### Traits
***Brave.*** The knight has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The knight makes two melee attacks.

***Greatsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** 2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. 5 (1d10) piercing damage.

***Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** For 1 minute, the knight can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature that it can see within 30 feet of it makes an attack roll or a saving throw. The creature can add a d4 to its roll provided it can hear and understand the knight. A creature can benefit from only one Leadership die at a time. This effect ends if the knight is [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The knight adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the knight must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
