### Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The bear has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bear makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Claws.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.
