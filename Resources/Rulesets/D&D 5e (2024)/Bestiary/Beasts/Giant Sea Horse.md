# Giant Sea Horse

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 16
**Speed**: walk 0 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 15 | 11 | 2 | 12 | 5 |

### Traits
***Charge.*** If the sea horse moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Water Breathing.*** The sea horse can breathe only underwater.

### Actions
***Ram.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.
