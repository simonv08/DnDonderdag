### Traits
***Charge.*** If the goat moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 10 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Sure-Footed.*** The goat has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
