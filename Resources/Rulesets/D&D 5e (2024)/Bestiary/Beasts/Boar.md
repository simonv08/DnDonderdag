# Boar

Medium Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 11
**Hit Points**: 11
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | 11 | 12 | 2 | 9 | 5 |

### Traits
***Charge.*** If the boar moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a tusk attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 3 (1d6) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Relentless (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** If the boar takes 7 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 hit points, it is reduced to 1 hit point instead.

### Actions
***Tusk.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage.
