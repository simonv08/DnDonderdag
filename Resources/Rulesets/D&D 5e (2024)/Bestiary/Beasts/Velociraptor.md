# Velociraptor

Tiny Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 10
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 14 | 13 | 4 | 12 | 6 |

### Traits
***Pack Tactics.*** The velociraptor has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the velociraptor's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The velociraptor makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.
