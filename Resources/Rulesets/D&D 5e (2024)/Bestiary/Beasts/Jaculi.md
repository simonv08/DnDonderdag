# Jaculi

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 16
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 20 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +4, Perception +1, Stealth +4
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft.
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 14 | 11 | 2 | 8 | 3 |

### Traits
***Camouflage.*** The jaculi has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide.

***Keen Smell.*** The jaculi has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Spring.*** The jaculi springs up to 30 feet in a straight line and makes a bite attack against a target within its reach. This attack has advantage if the jaculi springs at least 10 feet. If the attack hits, the bite deals an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage.
