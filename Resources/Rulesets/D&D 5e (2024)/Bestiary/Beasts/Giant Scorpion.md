# Giant Scorpion

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 52
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 13 | 15 | 1 | 9 | 3 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The scorpion makes three attacks: two with its claws and one with its sting.

***Claw.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 12). The scorpion has two claws, each of which can grapple only one target.

***Sting.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must make a 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
