### Traits
***Hold Breath.*** While out of water, the octopus can hold its breath for 1 hour.

***Underwater Camouflage.*** The octopus has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made while underwater.

***Water Breathing.*** The octopus can breathe only underwater.

### Actions
***Tentacles.*** 5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the octopus can't use its tentacles on another target.

***Ink Cloud (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** A 20-foot-radius cloud of ink extends all around the octopus if it is underwater. The area is Vision and Light for 1 minute, although a significant current can disperse the ink. After releasing the ink, the octopus can use the [[Actions#Dash|Dash]] action as a bonus action.
