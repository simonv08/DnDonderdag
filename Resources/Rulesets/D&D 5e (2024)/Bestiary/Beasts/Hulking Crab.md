Huge Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17

**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft.

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The crab can breathe air and water.

***Shell Camouflage.*** While the crab remains motionless with its eyestalks and pincers tucked close to its body, it resembles a natural formation or a pile of detritus. A creature within 30 feet of it can discern its true nature with a successful 15 Intelligence ([[Skills#Nature|Nature]]) check.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The crab makes two attacks with its claws.

***Claw.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 20 (3d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 15). The crab has two claws, each of which can grapple only one target
