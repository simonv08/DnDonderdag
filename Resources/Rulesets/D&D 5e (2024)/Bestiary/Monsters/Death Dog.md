### Traits
***Two-Headed.*** The dog has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks and on saving throws against being [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]], or knocked [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dog makes two bite attacks.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw against disease or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the disease is cured. Every 24 hours that elapse, the creature must repeat the saving throw, reducing its hit point maximum by 5 (1d10) on a failure. This reduction lasts until the disease is cured. The creature dies if the disease reduces its hit point maximum to 0.
