# Death Kiss

Large Aberration, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 161
**Speed**: walk 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Perception +5
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Deep Speech, Undercommon
**Challenge**: 10

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 14 | 18 | 10 | 12 | 10 |

### Traits
***Lightning Blood.*** A creature within 5 feet of the death kiss takes 5 (1d10) lightning damage whenever it hits the death kiss with a melee attack that deals piercing or slashing damage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The death kiss makes three tentacle attacks. Up to three of these attacks can be replaced by Blood Drain, one replacement per tentacle grappling a creature

***Tentacle.*** 8 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. 14 (3d6 + 4) piercing damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 14) if it is a Huge or smaller creature. Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the death kiss can't use the same tentacle on another target. The death kiss has ten tentacles.

***Blood Drain.*** One creature [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by a tentacle of the death kiss must make a 16 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 22 (4d10) lightning damage, and the death kiss regains half as many hit points.
