### Actions
***Bite.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw against being magically [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]]. On a failed save, the creature begins to turn to stone and is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]]. It must repeat the saving throw at the end of its next turn. On a success, the effect ends. On a failure, the creature is [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] for 24 hours.
