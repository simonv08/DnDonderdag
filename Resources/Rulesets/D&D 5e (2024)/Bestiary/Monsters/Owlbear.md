### Traits
***Keen Sight and Smell.*** The owlbear has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight or smell.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The owlbear makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its claws.

***Beak.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 10 (1d10 + 5) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.
