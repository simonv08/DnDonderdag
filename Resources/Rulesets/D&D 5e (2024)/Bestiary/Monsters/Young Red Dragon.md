# Young Red Dragon

Large Dragon, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 178
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., climb 40 ft., fly 80 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +8, Stealth +4
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 10

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) fire damage.

***Claw.*** 10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage.

***Fire Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales fire in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 56 (16d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
