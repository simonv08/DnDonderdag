# Purple Wormling

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 12
**Hit Points**: 42
**Speed**: walk 20 ft.
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft.
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 7 | 16 | 1 | 6 | 2 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The wormling makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its stinger.

***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 5) piercing damage. If the target is a Small or smaller creature, it must succeed on a 13 Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed by the worm. A swallowed creature is [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] and [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], it has Cover against attacks and other effects outside the wormling, and it takes 3 (1d6) acid damage at the start of each of the wormling's turns. If the wormling takes 10 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the wormling must succeed on a 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which fall [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] in a space within 10 feet of the wormling. If the wormling dies, a swallowed creature is no longer [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by it and can escape from the corpse by using 5 feet of movement, exiting [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Tail Stinger.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage, and the target must make a 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
