# The Lost

Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 78
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 12 | 15 | 6 | 7 | 5 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Lost makes two arm spike attacks.

***Arm Spike.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d10 + 3) piercing damage.

***Embrace.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 25 (4d10 + 3) piercing damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 14) if it is a Medium or smaller creature. Until the grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], and it takes 27 (6d8) psychic damage at the end of each of its turns. The Lost can embrace only one creature at a time.

### Reactions
***Tightening Embrace.*** If the Lost takes damage while it has a creature [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]], that creature takes 18 (4d8) psychic damage.
