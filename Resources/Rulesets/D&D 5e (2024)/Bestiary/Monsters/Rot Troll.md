# Rot Troll

Large Giant, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 138
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Giant
**Challenge**: 9

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 13 | 22 | 5 | 8 | 4 |

### Traits
***Rancid Degeneration.*** At the end of each of the troll's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 11 (2d10) necrotic damage, unless the troll has taken acid or fire damage since the end of its last turn.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The troll makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 16 (3d10) necrotic damage.

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) necrotic damage.
