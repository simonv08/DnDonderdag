# Dretch

Small Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 11
**Hit Points**: 18
**Speed**: walk 20 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Telepathy 60 ft. (works only with creatures that understand Abyssal)
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | 11 | 12 | 5 | 8 | 3 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dretch makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 3 (1d6) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (2d4) slashing damage.

***Fetid Cloud (1/Day).*** A 10-foot radius of disgusting green gas extends out from the dretch. The gas spreads around corners, and its area is lightly obscured. It lasts for 1 minute or until a strong wind disperses it. Any creature that starts its turn in that area must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the start of its next turn. While [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way, the target can take either an action or a bonus action on its turn, not both, and can't take reactions.
