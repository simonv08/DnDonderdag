# Githyanki Kith'rak

Medium Humanoid (Gith), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 180
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom
**Skills**: Intimidation +7, Perception +6
**Languages**: Gith
**Challenge**: 12

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 16 | 17 | 16 | 15 | 17 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting (Psionics).***
The githyanki's innate spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save 15, 7 to hit with spell attacks). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:
- **At will:** [[Mage Hand]] (the hand is invisible)
- **3/day each:** [[Blur]], [[Jump]], [[Misty Step]], [[Nondetection]] (self only)
- **1/day each:** [[Plane Shift]], [[Telekinesis]]

### Traits
***Rally the Troops.*** As a bonus action, the githyanki can magically end the [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] and [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] conditions on itself and each creature of its choice that it can see within 30 feet of it.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The githyanki makes three greatsword attacks.

***Greatsword.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 17 (5d6) psychic damage.

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The githyanki adds 4 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the githyanki must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
