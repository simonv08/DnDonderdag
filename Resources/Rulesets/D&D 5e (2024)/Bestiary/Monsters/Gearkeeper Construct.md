# Gearkeeper Construct

Large Construct, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 161
**Speed**: walk 60 ft.
**Senses**: Blindsight 120 ft.
**Languages**: Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
**Challenge**: 10

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 16 | 18 | 3 | 11 | 1 |

### Traits
***Immutable Form.*** The gearkeeper is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

***Rapid Shifting.*** Opportunity attacks made against the gearkeeper have disadvantage.

***Whirling Blades.*** Any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the gearkeeper takes 4 (1d8) slashing damage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gearkeeper makes two Arm Blade attacks, or one Arm Blade attack and one Spear Launcher attack.

***Arm Blade.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 18 (3d8 + 5) slashing damage.

***Spear Launcher.*** 9 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. 12 (2d6 + 5) piercing damage, and the target is knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Shrapnel Blast .*** The gearkeeper jettisons a spray of jagged metal in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
