# Core Spawn Seer

Medium Aberration, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 153
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +9
**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Deep Speech, Telepathy 120 ft., Undercommon
**Challenge**: 13

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 12 | 18 | 22 | 19 | 16 |

### Traits
***Earth Glide.*** The seer can traverse through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone. While doing so, the seer doesn't disturb the material it moves through.

***Magic Resistance.*** The seer has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The seer uses Fission Staff twice, Psychedelic Orb twice, or each one once.

***Fission Staff.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 9 (1d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) radiant damage, and the target is knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Psychedelic Orb.*** The seer hurls a glimmering orb at one creature it can see within 120 of it. The target must succeed on a 19 Wisdom saving throw or take 27 (5d10) psychic damage and suffer a random condition until the start of the seer's next turn. Roll a d6 for the condition: (1-2) [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], (3-4) [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], or (5-6) [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]].

### Reactions
***Fuse Damage.*** When the seer is hit by an attack, it takes only half of the triggering damage. The first time the seer hits with a melee attack on its next turn, the target takes an extra 1d6 radiant damage.
