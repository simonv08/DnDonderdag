# Inquisitor of the Mind Fire

Medium Humanoid, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 77
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Insight +6, Perception +6
**Senses**: Truesight 30 ft.
**Languages**: Any three languages, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 8

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 14 | 12 | 17 | 16 | 19 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting (Psionics).***
The inquisitor casts one of the following spells, requiring no components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save 15):
- **At will:** [[Arcane Eye]], [[Calm Emotions]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Detect Thoughts]], [[Dispel Magic]], [[Sending]], [[Suggestion]]
- **1/day each:** [[Mass Suggestion]], [[Modify Memory]]

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The inquisitor attacks twice with its Silver Longsword or uses Mind Fire twice.

***Silver Longsword.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) if used with two hands, plus 18 (4d8) force damage.

***Mind Fire.*** The inquisitor targets one creature it can see within 120 feet of it. The target must succeed on a 15 Intelligence saving throw or take 17 (3d8 + 4) psychic damage and be [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]] until the start of the inquisitor's next turn.

***Inquisitor's Command (recharge: 5).*** Each creature of the inquisitor's choice that it can see within 60 feet of it must succeed on a 15 Wisdom saving throw or be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] until the start of the inquisitor's next turn. On the [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] target's turn, the inquisitor can telepathically control the target's move, action, or both. When controlled in this way, the target can take only the Attack (inquisitor chooses the target) or [[Actions#Dash|Dash]] action.
