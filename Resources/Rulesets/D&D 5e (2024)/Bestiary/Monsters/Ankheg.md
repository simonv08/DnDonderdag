# Ankheg

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 39
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 11 | 13 | 1 | 13 | 6 |

### Actions
***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 13). Until this grapple ends, the ankheg can bite only the [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] creature and has advantage on attack rolls to do so.

***Acid Spray .*** The ankheg spits acid in a line that is 30 feet long and 5 feet wide, provided that it has no creature [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]]. Each creature in that line must make a 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
