Medium Dragon, Chaotic Evil

**Hit Points**: 32

**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Draconic
**Challenge**: 2

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage.

***Cold Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales an icy blast of hail in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 22 (5d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
