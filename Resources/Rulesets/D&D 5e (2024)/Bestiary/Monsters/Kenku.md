### Traits
***Ambusher.*** In the first round of a combat, the kenku has advantage on attack rolls against any creature it [[Statuss#surprised|Surprised]].

***Mimicry.*** The kenku can mimic any sounds it has heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful 14 Wisdom ([[Skills#Insight|Insight]]) check.
