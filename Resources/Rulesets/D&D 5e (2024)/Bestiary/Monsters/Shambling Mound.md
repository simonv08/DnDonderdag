# Shambling Mound

Large Plant, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 136
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., swim 20 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius)
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 8 | 16 | 5 | 10 | 5 |

### Traits
***Lightning Absorption.*** Whenever the shambling mound is subjected to lightning damage, it takes no damage and regains a number of hit points equal to the lightning damage dealt.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The shambling mound makes two slam attacks. If both attacks hit a Medium or smaller target, the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 14), and the shambling mound uses its Engulf on it.

***Slam.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Engulf.*** The shambling mound engulfs a Medium or smaller creature [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by it. The engulfed target is [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and unable to breathe, and it must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw at the start of each of the mound's turns or take 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the mound moves, the engulfed target moves with it. The mound can have only one creature engulfed at a time.
