# Marid

Large Elemental, Chaotic Neutral

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 229
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 90 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Aquan
**Challenge**: 11

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The marid's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 16, 8 to hit with spell attacks). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Create Or Destroy Water]], [[Detect Evil And Good]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Fog Cloud]], [[Purify Food And Drink]]
- **3/day each:** [[Tongues]], [[Water Breathing]], [[Water Walk]]
- **1/day each:** [[Conjure Elemental]] ([[Water Elemental]] only), [[Control Water]], [[Gaseous Form]], [[Invisibility]], [[Plane Shift]]

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The marid can breathe air and water.

***Elemental Demise.*** If the marid dies, its body disintegrates into a burst of water and foam, leaving behind only equipment the marid was wearing or carrying.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The marid makes two trident attacks.

***Trident.*** 10 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 13 (2d6 + 6) piercing damage, or 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Water Jet.*** The marid magically shoots water in a 60-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a 16 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage and, if it is Huge or smaller, is pushed up to 20 feet away from the marid and knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. On a success, a target takes half the bludgeoning damage, but is neither pushed nor knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
