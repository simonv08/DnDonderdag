### Traits
***Incorporeal Movement.*** The demon can move through other creatures and objects as if they were [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]]. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

***Light Sensitivity.*** While in bright light, the demon has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

***Shadow Stealth.*** While in dim light or darkness, the demon can take the [[Actions#Hide|Hide]] action as a bonus action.

### Actions
***Claws.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 10 (2d6 + 3) psychic damage or, if the demon had advantage on the attack roll, 17 (4d6 + 3) psychic damage.
