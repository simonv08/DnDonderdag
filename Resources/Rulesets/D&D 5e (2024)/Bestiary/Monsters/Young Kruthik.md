# Young Kruthik

Small Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 9
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., burrow 10 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 30 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Kruthik
**Challenge**: 1/8

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | 16 | 13 | 4 | 10 | 6 |

### Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The kruthik has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The kruthik has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kruthik's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Tunneler.*** The kruthik can burrow through solid rock at half its burrowing speed and leaves a 2½-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.

### Actions
***Stab.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
