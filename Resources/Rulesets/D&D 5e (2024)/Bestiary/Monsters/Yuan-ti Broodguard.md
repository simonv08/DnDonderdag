# Yuan-ti Broodguard

Medium Humanoid (Yuan-ti), Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 45
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom
**Skills**: Perception +2
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 14 | 14 | 6 | 11 | 4 |

### Traits
***Mental Resistance.*** The broodguard has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't paralyze it.

***Reckless.*** At the start of its turn, the broodguard can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls it makes during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The broodguard makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
