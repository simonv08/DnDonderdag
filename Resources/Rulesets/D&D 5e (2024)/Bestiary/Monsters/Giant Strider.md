# Giant Strider

Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 22
**Speed**: walk 50 ft.
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 13 | 14 | 4 | 12 | 6 |

### Traits
***Fire Absorption.*** Whenever the giant strider is subjected to fire damage, it takes no damage and regains a number of hit points equal to half the fire damage dealt.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

***Fire Burst (recharge: 5).*** The giant strider hurls a gout of flame at a point it can see within 60 feet of it. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The fire spreads around corners, and it ignites flammable objects in that area that aren't being worn or carried.
