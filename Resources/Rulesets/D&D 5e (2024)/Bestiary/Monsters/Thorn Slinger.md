### Traits
***Adhesive Blossoms.*** The thorn slinger adheres to anything that touches it. A Medium or smaller creature adhered to the thorn slinger is also [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by it (escape 11). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have disadvantage. At the end of each of the thorn slinger's turns, anything [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by it takes 3 (1d6) acid damage.

***False Appearance.*** While the thorn slinger remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate bush.

### Actions
***Thorns.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30 ft., one target. 8 (2d6 + 1) piercing damage.
