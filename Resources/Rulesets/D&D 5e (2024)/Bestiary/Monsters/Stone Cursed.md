# Stone Cursed

Medium Construct, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 19
**Speed**: walk 10 ft.
**Languages**: The languages it knew in life
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 5 | 14 | 5 | 8 | 7 |

### Traits
***Cunning Opportunist.*** The stone cursed has advantage on the attack rolls of opportunity attacks.

***False Appearance.*** While the stone cursed remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal statue.

### Actions
***Petrifying Claws.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage, or 14 (2d10 + 3) slashing damage if the attack roll had advantage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw, or it begins to turn to stone and is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] until the end of its next turn, when it must repeat the saving throw. The effect ends if the second save is successful; otherwise the target is [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] for 24 hours.
