# Armanite

Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 84
**Speed**: walk 60 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | 18 | 21 | 8 | 12 | 13 |

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The armanite has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** The armanite's weapon attacks are magical.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The armanite makes three attacks: one with its hooves, one with its claws, and one with its serrated tail.

***Hooves.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d4 + 5) slashing damage.

***Serrated Tail.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 16 (2d10 + 5) slashing damage.

***Lightning Lance (recharge: 5).*** The armanite looses a bolt of lightning in a line 60 feet long and 10 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
