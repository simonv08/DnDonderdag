# Sprite

Tiny Fey, Neutral Good

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 2
**Speed**: walk 10 ft., fly 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +8
**Languages**: Common, Elvish, Sylvan
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 18 | 10 | 14 | 13 | 11 |

### Actions
***Longsword.*** 2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 1 slashing damage.

***Shortbow.*** 6 to hit, range 40/160 ft., one target. 1 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 10 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. If its saving throw result is 5 or lower, the [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] target falls [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] for the same duration, or until it takes damage or another creature takes an action to shake it awake.

***Heart Sight.*** The sprite touches a creature and magically knows the creature's current emotional state. If the target fails a 10 Charisma saving throw, the sprite also knows the creature's alignment. Celestials, fiends, and undead automatically fail the saving throw.

***Invisibility.*** The sprite magically turns [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] until it attacks or casts a spell, or until its [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] ends (as if [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on a spell). Any equipment the sprite wears or carries is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] with it.
