# Green Dragon Wyrmling

Medium Dragon, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 38
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +3
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Draconic
**Challenge**: 2

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The dragon can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage.

***Poison Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales poisonous gas in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
