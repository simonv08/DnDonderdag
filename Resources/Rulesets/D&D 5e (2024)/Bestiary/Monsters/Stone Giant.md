# Stone Giant

Huge Giant, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 126
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Athletics +12, Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Giant
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 23 | 15 | 20 | 10 | 12 | 9 |

### Traits
***Stone Camouflage.*** The giant has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two greatclub attacks.

***Greatclub.*** 9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** 9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Reactions
***Rock Catching.*** If a rock or similar object is hurled at the giant, the giant can, with a successful 10 Dexterity saving throw, catch the missile and take no bludgeoning damage from it.
