# Galeb Duhr

Medium Elemental, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 15 ft. (30 ft. when rolling, 60 ft. rolling downhill)
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Terran
**Challenge**: 6

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 14 | 20 | 11 | 12 | 11 |

### Traits
***False Appearance.*** While the galeb duhr remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal boulder.

***Rolling Charge.*** If the galeb duhr rolls at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a slam attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Slam.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

***Animate Boulders (1/Day).*** The galeb duhr magically animates up to two boulders it can see within 60 feet of it. A boulder has statistics like those of a galeb duhr, except it has Intelligence 1 and Charisma 1, it can't be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], and it lacks this action option. A boulder remains animated as long as the galeb duhr maintains [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute (as if [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on a spell).
