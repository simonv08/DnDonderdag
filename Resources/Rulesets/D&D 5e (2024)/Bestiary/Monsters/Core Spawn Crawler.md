### Traits
***Pack Tactics.*** The crawler has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the crawler's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The crawler makes four attacks: one with its bite, two with its claws, and one with its tail.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage and the target must succeed on a 11 Wisdom saving throw or become [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] until the start of the crawler's next turn.

***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

***Tail.*** 4 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
