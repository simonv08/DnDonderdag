### Traits
***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the kobold has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

***Pack Tactics.*** The kobold has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Dagger.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

***Sling.*** 4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
