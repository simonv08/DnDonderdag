# Swavain Basilisk

Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 15 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 16 | 15 | 2 | 8 | 7 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The basilisk can breathe air and water.

***Petrifying Secretions.*** A creature must make a 13 Constitution saving throw if it hits the basilisk with a weapon attack while within 5 feet of it or if it starts its turn [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by the basilisk. Unless the save succeeds, the creature magically begins to turn to stone and is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and it must repeat the saving throw at the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the effect ends. On a failure, the creature is [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The basilisk makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 13 (3d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) poison damage.

***Tail.*** 6 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 14 (2d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 12).
