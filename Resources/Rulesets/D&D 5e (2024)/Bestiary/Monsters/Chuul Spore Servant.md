***Pincer.*** 6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. The target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (Escape 14) if it is a Large or smaller creature and the spore servant doesn't have two other creatures [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]].
