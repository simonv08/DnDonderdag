# Young Green Dragon

Large Dragon, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 136
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Deception +5, Perception +7, Stealth +4
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 8

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The dragon can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

***Claw.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Poison Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales poisonous gas in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
