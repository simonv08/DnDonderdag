# Dragonclaw

Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 16
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Wisdom
**Skills**: Deception +3, Stealth +5
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 1

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Dragon Fanatic.*** The dragonclaw has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]]. While the dragonclaw can see a dragon or higher-ranking Cult of the Dragon cultist friendly to it, the dragonclaw ignores the effects of being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].

***Fanatic Advantage.*** Once per turn, if the dragonclaw makes a weapon attack with advantage on the attack roll and hits, it deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage.

***Pack Tactics.*** The dragonclaw has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the dragonclaw's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragonclaw attacks twice with its scimitar.

***Scimitar.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
