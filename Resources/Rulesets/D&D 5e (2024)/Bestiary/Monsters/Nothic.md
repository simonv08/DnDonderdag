# Nothic

Medium Aberration, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 45
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.

**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.

**Challenge**: 2

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Keen Sight.*** The nothic has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The nothic makes two claw attacks.

***Claw.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

***Rotting Gaze.*** The nothic targets one creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The target must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw against this magic or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

***Weird Insight.*** The nothic targets one creature it can see within 30 feet of it. The target must contest its Charisma ([[Skills#Deception|Deception]]) check against the nothic's Wisdom ([[Skills#Insight|Insight]]) check. If the nothic wins, it magically learns one fact or secret about the target. The target automatically wins if it is immune to being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]].
