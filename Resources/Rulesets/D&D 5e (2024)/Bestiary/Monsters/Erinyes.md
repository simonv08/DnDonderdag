# Erinyes

Medium Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 153
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.
**Languages**: Infernal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 12

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 16 | 18 | 14 | 14 | 18 |

### Traits
***Hellish Weapons.*** The erinyes's weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra 13 (3d8) poison damage on a hit (included in the attacks).

***Magic Resistance.*** The erinyes has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The erinyes makes three attacks.

***Longsword.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage if used with two hands, plus 13 (3d8) poison damage.

***Longbow.*** 7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]]. The poison lasts until it is removed by the [[Lesser Restoration]] spell or similar magic.

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The erinyes adds 4 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the erinyes must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
