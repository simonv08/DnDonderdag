# Gray Render

Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 189
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Constitution
**Skills**: Perception +2
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 12

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 13 | 20 | 3 | 6 | 8 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gray render makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 17 (2d12 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, the target must succeed on a 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] an additional 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage is dealt to the target.

### Reactions
***Bloody Rampage.*** When the gray render takes damage, it makes one attack with its claws against a random creature within its reach, other than its master.
