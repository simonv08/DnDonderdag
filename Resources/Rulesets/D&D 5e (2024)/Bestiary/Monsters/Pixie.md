### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The pixie's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 12). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring only its pixie dust as a component:
- **At will:** [[Druidcraft]]
- **1/day each:** [[Confusion]], [[Dancing Lights]], [[Detect Evil And Good]], [[Detect Thoughts]], [[Dispel Magic]], [[Entangle]], [[Fly]], [[Phantasmal Force]], [[Polymorph]], [[Sleep]]

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The pixie has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Superior Invisibility.*** The pixie magically turns [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] until its [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] ends (as if [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on a spell). Any equipment the pixie wears or carries is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] with it.
