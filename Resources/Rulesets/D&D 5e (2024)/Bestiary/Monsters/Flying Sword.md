Small Construct, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 17
**Speed**: walk 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)

**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius)

### Traits
***Antimagic Susceptibility.*** The sword is [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] while in the area of an [[Antimagic Field]]. If targeted by [[Dispel Magic]], the sword must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] for 1 minute.

***False Appearance.*** While the sword remains motionless and isn't flying, it is indistinguishable from a normal sword.

### Actions
***Longsword.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage.
