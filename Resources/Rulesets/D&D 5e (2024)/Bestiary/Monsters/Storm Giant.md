Huge Giant, Chaotic Good

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 230
**Speed**: walk 50 ft., swim 50 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Arcana +8, Athletics +14, History +8, Perception +9

**Challenge**: 13

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The giant's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 17). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Feather Fall]], [[Levitate]], [[Light]]
- **3/day each:** [[Control Weather]], [[Water Breathing]]

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The giant can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two greatsword attacks.

***Greatsword.*** 14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 30 (6d6 + 9) slashing damage.

***Rock.*** 14 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. 35 (4d12 + 9) bludgeoning damage.

***Lightning Strike (recharge: 5).*** The giant hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point it can see within 500 feet of it. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
