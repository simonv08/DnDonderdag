### Actions
***Multiattack (Lizardfolk Form Only).*** The lizardfolk makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage in [[Crocodile]] form. If the lizardfolk is in crocodile form and the target is a Large or smaller creature, the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 12). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the lizardfolk can't bite another target. If the lizardfolk reverts to its true form, the grapple ends.

***Claws (Lizardfolk Form Only).*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

***Change Shape (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The lizardfolk magically polymorphs into a [[Crocodile]], remaining in that form for up to 1 hour. It can revert to its true form as a bonus action. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.
