# Merrow

Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 45
**Speed**: walk 10 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Aquan
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 10 | 15 | 8 | 10 | 9 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The merrow can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The merrow makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws or harpoon.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing damage.

***Harpoon.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it must succeed on a Strength contest against the merrow or be pulled up to 20 feet toward the merrow.
