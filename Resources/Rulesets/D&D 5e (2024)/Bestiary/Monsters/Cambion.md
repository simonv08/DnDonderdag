# Cambion

Medium Fiend, Lawful NX Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 82
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Constitution, Intelligence, Charisma
**Skills**: Deception +6, Intimidation +6, Perception +4, Stealth +7
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Common, Infernal
**Challenge**: 5

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The cambion's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 14). The cambion can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **1/day:** [[Plane Shift]] (self only)
- **3/day each:** [[Alter Self]], [[Command]], [[Detect Magic]]

### Traits
***Fiendish Blessing.*** The AC of the cambion includes its Charisma bonus.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The cambion makes two melee attacks or uses its Fire Ray twice.

***Spear.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage, or 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack, plus 3 (1d6) fire damage.

***Fire Ray.*** 7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. 10 (3d6) fire damage.

***Fiendish Charm.*** One humanoid the cambion can see within 30 feet of it must succeed on a 14 Wisdom saving throw or be magically [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] for 1 day. The [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] target obeys the cambion's spoken commands. If the target suffers any harm from the cambion or another creature or receives a suicidal command from the cambion, the target can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful, or if the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the cambion's Fiendish Charm for the next 24 hours.
