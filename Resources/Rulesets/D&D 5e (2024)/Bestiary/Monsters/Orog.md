**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.

### Traits
***Aggressive.*** As a bonus action, the orog can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The orog makes two greataxe attacks.

***Greataxe.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (1d12 + 4) slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
