# Chitine

Small Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 18
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +4, Stealth +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Undercommon
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 14 | 12 | 10 | 10 | 7 |

### Traits
***Fey Ancestry.*** The chitine has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't put the chitine to sleep.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the chitine has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

***Web Sense.*** While in contact with a web, the chitine knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

***Web Walker.*** The chitine ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The chitine makes three attacks with its daggers.

***Dagger.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
