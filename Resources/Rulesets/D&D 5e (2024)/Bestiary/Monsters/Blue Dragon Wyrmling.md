# Blue Dragon Wyrmling

Medium Dragon, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 52
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., burrow 15 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Draconic
**Challenge**: 3

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Actions
***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) lightning damage.

***Lightning Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales lightning in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
