# Giant Ice Toad

Large Monstrosity, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 52
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Ice Toad
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 13 | 14 | 8 | 10 | 6 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The toad can breathe air and water.

***Cold Aura.*** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the toad takes 5 (1d10) cold damage.

***Standing Leap.*** The toad's long jump is up to 20 feet and its high jump is up to 10 feet, with or without a running start.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 13). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the toad can't bite another target.

***Swallow.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Medium or smaller creature the toad is grappling. 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage, the target is swallowed, and the grapple ends. The swallowed target is [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] and [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], it has Cover against attacks and other effects outside the toad, and it takes 10 (3d6) acid damage and 11 (2d10) cold damage at the start of each of the toad's turns. The toad can have only one target swallowed at a time. If the toad dies, a swallowed creature is no longer [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by it and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
