# Xorn

Medium Elemental, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 73
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., burrow 20 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +6, Stealth +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Terran
**Challenge**: 5

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Earth Glide.*** The xorn can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone. While doing so, the xorn doesn't disturb the material it moves through.

***Stone Camouflage.*** The xorn has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

***Treasure Sense.*** The xorn can pinpoint, by scent, the location of precious metals and stones, such as coins and gems, within 60 feet of it.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The xorn makes three claw attacks and one bite attack.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (3d6 + 3) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
