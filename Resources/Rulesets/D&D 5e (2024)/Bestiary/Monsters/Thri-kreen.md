# Thri-kreen

Medium Humanoid (Thri-kreen), Chaotic Neutral

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 33
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +4, Survival +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Thri-kreen
**Challenge**: 1

### Traits
***Chameleon Carapace.*** The thri-kreen can change the color of its carapace to match the color and texture of its surroundings. As a result, it has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide.

***Standing Leap.*** The thri-kreen's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The thri-kreen makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Claws.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.
