### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting (1/Day).***
The mephit can innately cast [[Dancing Lights]], requiring no material components. Its innate spellcasting ability is Charisma.
- **At will:** [[Dancing Lights]]

### Traits
***Death Burst.*** When the mephit dies, it leaves behind a cloud of smoke that fills a 5-foot-radius sphere centered on its space. The sphere is Vision and Light. Wind disperses the cloud, which otherwise lasts for 1 minute.

### Actions
***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

***Cinder Breath .*** The mephit exhales a 15-foot cone of smoldering ash. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 10 Dexterity saving throw or be [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] until the end of the mephit's next turn.
