# Will-o'-Wisp

Tiny Undead, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: undefined
**Hit Points**: 22
**Speed**: walk 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: The languages it knew in life
**Challenge**: 2

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 28 | 10 | 13 | 14 | 11 |

### Traits
***Consume Life.*** As a bonus action, the will-o'-wisp can target one creature it can see within 5 feet of it that has 0 hit points and is still alive. The target must succeed on a 10 Constitution saving throw against this magic or die. If the target dies, the will-o'-wisp regains 10 (3d6) hit points.

***Ephemeral.*** The will-o'-wisp can't wear or carry anything.

***Incorporeal Movement.*** The will-o'-wisp can move through other creatures and objects as if they were [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]]. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

***Variable Illumination.*** The will-o'-wisp sheds bright light in a 5 to 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional number of ft. equal to the chosen radius. The will-o'-wisp can alter the radius as a bonus action.

### Actions
***Shock.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 9 (2d8) lightning damage.

***Invisibility.*** The will-o'-wisp and its light magically become [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] until it attacks or uses its Consume Life, or until its [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] ends (as if [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on a spell).
