# Carrion Crawler

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 51
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 13 | 16 | 1 | 12 | 5 |

### Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The carrion crawler has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

***Spider Climb.*** The carrion crawler can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The carrion crawler makes two attacks: one with its tentacles and one with its bite.

***Tentacles.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. 4 (1d4 + 2) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. Until this poison ends, the target is [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]]. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the poison on itself on a success.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage.
