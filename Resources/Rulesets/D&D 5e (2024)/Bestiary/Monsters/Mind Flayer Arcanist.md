### Actions
***Tentacles.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 15 (2d10 + 4) psychic damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 15) and must succeed on a 15 Intelligence saving throw or be [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]] until this grapple ends.

***Extract Brain.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] humanoid [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by the mind flayer. The target takes 55 (10d10) piercing damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the mind flayer kills the target by extracting and devouring its brain.

***Mind Blast (recharge: 5).*** The mind flayer magically emits psychic energy in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 15 Intelligence saving throw or take 22 (4d8 + 4) psychic damage and be [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]] for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
