# Duergar Kavalrachni

Medium Humanoid (Dwarf), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 26
**Speed**: walk 25 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Dwarvish, Undercommon
**Challenge**: 2

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 11 | 14 | 11 | 10 | 9 |

### Traits
***Cavalry Training.*** When the duergar hits a target with a melee attack while mounted on a female steeder, the steeder can make one melee attack against the same target as a reaction.

***Duergar Resilience.*** The duergar has advantage on saving throws against poison, spells, and illusions, as well as to resist being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]].

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the duergar has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The duergar makes two war pick attacks.

***War Pick.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage plus 5 (2d4) poison damage.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** 2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. 5 (1d10) piercing damage.

***Shared Invisibility (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** The duergar magically turns [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] for up to 1 hour or until it attacks, it casts a spell, or its [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] is broken (as if [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on a spell). Any equipment the duergar wears or carries is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] with it. While the [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] duergar is mounted on a female steeder, the steeder is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] as well. The invisibility ends early on the steeder immediately after it attacks.
