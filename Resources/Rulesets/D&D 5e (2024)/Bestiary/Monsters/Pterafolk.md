# Pterafolk

Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 12
**Hit Points**: 26
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 50 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +2, Survival +2
**Languages**: Common
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 13 | 12 | 9 | 10 | 11 |

### Traits
***Terror Dive.*** If the pterafolk is flying and dives at least 30 feet straight toward a target, and then hits that target with a melee weapon attack, the target is [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] until the end of its next turn.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The pterafolk makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws. Alternatively, it makes two melee attacks with its javelin.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.
