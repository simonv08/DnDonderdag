# Guardian Wolf

Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 66
**Speed**: walk 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +5, Stealth +4
**Languages**: Common, Elvish
**Challenge**: 4

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22 | 14 | 16 | 5 | 12 | 8 |

### Traits
***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The wolf has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The wolf makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (1d10 + 6) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage.
