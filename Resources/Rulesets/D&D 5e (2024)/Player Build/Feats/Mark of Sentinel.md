# Mark of Sentinel

You gain the following benefits.

## Sentinel's Intuition

When you make a Wisdom ([[Skills#Insight|Insight]] or [[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) check, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to the ability check.
