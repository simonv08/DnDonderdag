# Greater Mark of Shadow

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Mark Of Shadow]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Improved Intuition

When you use the Cunning Intuition benefit of your  feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

## Improved Shadow

When you use the Shape Shadows benefit of your  feat to cast [[Invisibility]] without a spell slot, you cast the spell as its level 3 version.
