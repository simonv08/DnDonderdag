# Greater Mark of Storm

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Mark Of Storm]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Improved Intuition

When you use the Windwright's Intuition benefit of your  feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

## Improved Storm

When you use the Storm Magic benefit of your  feat to cast [[Gust Of Wind]] without a spell slot, you also gain a  of 60 feet for the duration of the spell.
