# Greater Mark of Detection

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Mark Of Detection]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Improved Intuition

When you use the Deductive Intuition benefit of your  feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

## Shared Detection

When you use the Magical Detection benefit of your  feat to cast [[See Invisibility]] without a spell slot, you can choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself. That creature also gains the benefits of the spell for its duration.
