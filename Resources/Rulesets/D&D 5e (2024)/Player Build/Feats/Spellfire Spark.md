# Spellfire Spark

You gain the following benefits.

## Magic Absorption

Once per turn, when you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you reduce the total damage taken by 1d4. You can't use this benefit if you have the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
