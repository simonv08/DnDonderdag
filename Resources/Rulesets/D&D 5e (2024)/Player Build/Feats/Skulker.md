# Skulker

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4

You gain the following benefits.

## Blindsight

You have Blindsight|XPHB with a range of 10 feet.

## Fog of War

You exploit the distractions of battle, gaining  on any Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) check you make as part of the [[Actions#Hide|Hide]] action during combat.

## Sniper

If you make an attack roll while hidden and the roll misses, making the attack roll doesn't reveal your location.
