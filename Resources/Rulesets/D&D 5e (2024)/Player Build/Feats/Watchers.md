# Watchers

Something unnatural is always watching you, taking the form of scurrying vermin and other eerie creatures. You gain the following features.

## Heightened Suspicion

Whenever you take the [[Actions#Search|Search]] action, you can roll 1d4 and add the number rolled to any ability check made as part of that action.
