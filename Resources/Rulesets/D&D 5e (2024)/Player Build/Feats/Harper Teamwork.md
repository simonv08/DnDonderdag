# Harper Teamwork

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Harper Agent]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Withering Wordplay

When you take the [[Actions#Help|Help]] action to assist an ally's attack roll against an enemy, that enemy also has  on the first saving throw it makes before the start of your next turn.

## Inspiring Willpower

If you succeed on a saving throw to end the [[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]] or [[Conditions#Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition on yourself, you can choose one ally you can see within 30 feet of yourself that has the same condition. That condition immediately ends for that ally.
