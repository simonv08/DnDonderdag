# Order's Resilience

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Tyro Of The Gauntlet]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Resurge

When you have the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition, you can right yourself with only 5 feet of movement.

## Stronger Together

If you are within 5 feet of an ally that doesn't have the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, you and that ally have  on Strength saving throws. You can't use this benefit while you have the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
