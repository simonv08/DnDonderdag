# Boon of Dimensional Travel

**Prerequisites**
- Level 19

You gain the following benefits.

## Blink Steps

Immediately after you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action or the [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
