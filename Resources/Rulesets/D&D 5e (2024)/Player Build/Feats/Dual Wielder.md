# Dual Wielder

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Level 4

You gain the following benefits.

## Enhanced Dual Wielding

When you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a weapon that has the  property, you can make one extra attack as a  later on the same turn with a different weapon, which must be a Melee weapon that lacks the  property. You don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative.

## Quick Draw

You can draw or stow two weapons that lack the  property when you would normally be able to draw or stow only one.
