# Boon of Spell Recall

**Prerequisites**
- Level 19

You gain the following benefits.

## Free Casting

Whenever you cast a spell with a level 1-4 spell slot, roll 1d4. If the number you roll is the same as the slot's level, the slot isn't expended.
