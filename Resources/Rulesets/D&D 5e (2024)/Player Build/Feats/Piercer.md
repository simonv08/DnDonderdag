# Piercer

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4

You gain the following benefits.

## Puncture

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack that deals Piercing damage, you can reroll one of the attack's damage dice, and you must use the new roll.

## Enhanced Critical

When you score a  that deals Piercing damage to a creature, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the extra Piercing damage the target takes.
