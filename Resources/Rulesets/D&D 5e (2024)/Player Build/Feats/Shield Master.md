# Shield Master

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4

You gain the following benefits.

## Shield Bash

If you attack a creature within 5 feet of you as part of the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action and hit with a Melee weapon, you can immediately bash the target with your [[Shield]] if it's equipped, forcing the target to make a Strength saving throw (8 plus your Strength modifier and ). On a failed save, you either push the target 5 feet from you or cause it to have the [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]] condition (your choice). You can use this benefit only once on each of your turns.

## Interpose Shield

If you're subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you can take a  to take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw and are holding a [[Shield]].
