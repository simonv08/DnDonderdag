# Greater Mark of Finding

**Prerequisites**
- Level 4
- Prerequisite: [[Mark Of Finding]]

You gain the following benefits.

## Improved Intuition

When you use the Hunter's Intuition benefit of your  feat, you can roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

## Improved Finding

When you use the Finder's Magic benefit of your  to cast [[Hunter's Mark]] without a spell slot, the range of the spell is doubled, and you can modify the spell so that the target can't benefit from the [[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]] condition for the duration.
