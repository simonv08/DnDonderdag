# Scion of the Three

**Parent Class:** [[Rogue]]

**Source:** FRHoF

A Scion of the Three draws power from a group of malevolent gods known as the Dead Three: , deity of tyranny; , deity of violence and murder; and , deity of death. While some Rogues of this subclass pledge themselves ardently to those three macabre gods, others find themselves thrust on this path by a curse. Either way, a scion's power manifests as various occult gifts, as well as an uncanny talent for striking and terrifying foes.

Scions of the Three are most common in Baldur's Gate, where the Dead Three have long competed for influence over mortal hearts.
