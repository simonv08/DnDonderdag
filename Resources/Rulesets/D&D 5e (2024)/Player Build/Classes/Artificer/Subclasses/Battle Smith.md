# Battle Smith

**Parent Class:** [[Artificer]]

**Source:** EFA

A Battle Smith is a combination of protector and medic, an expert at defending others and repairing both materiel and personnel. To aid in their work, Battle Smiths are accompanied by a [[Steel Defender]], a protective companion of their own creation.

---

### Battle Ready

Your combat training and your experiments with magic have paid off in two ways.

##### Arcane Empowerment

When you attack with a magic weapon, you can use your Intelligence modifier, instead of your Strength or Dexterity modifier, for the attack and damage rolls.

### Steel Defender

Your tinkering has borne you a companion, a [[Steel Defender]]. You determine the defender's appearance and whether it has two legs or four; your choices don't affect the defender's game statistics.

The [[Steel Defender]] is  to you and your allies and obeys you. It vanishes if you die.

##### The Defender in Combat

In combat, the defender acts during your turn. It can move and take its  on its own, but the only action it takes is the [[Actions#Dodge|Dodge]] action unless you take a  to command it to take an action. If you have the [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, the defender acts on its own and isn't limited to the [[Actions#Dodge|Dodge]] action.

If the defender has died within the last hour, you can take a [[Actions#Magic|Magic]] action to touch it and expend a spell slot. The defender returns to life after 1 minute with all its  restored.

Whenever you finish a , you can create a new [[Steel Defender]] if you have [[Smith's Tools]] in hand. If you already have a defender from this feature, the first one vanishes.

### Tools of the Trade

You gain the following benefits.

##### Tool Proficiency

You gain proficiency with [[Smith's Tools]]. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of [[Artisan's Tools]] of your choice.

##### Weapon Crafting

When you craft a nonmagical or magic weapon, the amount of time required to craft it is halved.

## Level 5

### Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action on your turn. You can forgo one of your attacks when you take the [[Actions#Attack|Attack]] action to command your [[Steel Defender]] to take the Force-Empowered Rend action.

## Level 15

### Improved Defender

Your  and [[Steel Defender]] have become more powerful, granting these benefits.

##### Improved Jolt

The extra damage and healing of your Arcane Jolt both increase to 4d6.

##### Improved Deflection
