# Aven
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 25 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** DEX +2, WIS +2
**Languages:** Common, Other
## Traits
##### Alignment
Aven are inclined toward the lawful good alignment of the Church of Serra
##### Flight
You have a flying speed of 30 feet. You can't use your flying speed while you wear medium or heavy armor. (If your campaign uses the variant rule for , you can't use your flying speed if you are encumbered.) 
##### Hawkeyed
You have proficiency in the [[Skills#Perception|Perception]] skill. In addition, attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage on your ranged weapon attack rolls.
