# Skeleton
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** DEX +2, INT -4, CHA -4
## Traits
##### Creature Type
You are Undead.
##### Brittle Bones
You are vulnerable to bludgeoning damage.
##### Undead Nature
You are immune to poison damage and [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]], and you can't be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]]. You don't require air, food, drink, or sleep.
##### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
