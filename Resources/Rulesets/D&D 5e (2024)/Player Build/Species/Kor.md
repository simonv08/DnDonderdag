# Kor
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** DEX +2, WIS +1
**Languages:** Common, Other
## Traits
##### Alignment
Most kor are lawful good, with a strong dedication to community and the traditions of their ancestors.
##### Brave
You have advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].
##### Climbing
You also have a climbing speed of 30 feet as long as you are not encumbered or wearing heavy armor.
##### Kor Climbing
You have proficiency in the [[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]] and [[Skills#Acrobatics|Acrobatics]] skills.
##### Lucky
When you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
