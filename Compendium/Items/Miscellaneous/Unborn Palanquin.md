# Unborn Palanquin

The Unborn Palanquin is a wormlike leviathan of screeching gears made up of hundreds of tireless [[Mezzoloth]]. It is a unique vehicle which serves as both [[Malaxxix]]'s primary conveyance and the Fiend's lair.
