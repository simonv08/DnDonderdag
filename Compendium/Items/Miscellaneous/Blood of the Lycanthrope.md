# Blood of the Lycanthrope

This poison is created from blood harvested from a dead or [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] lycanthrope in its animal or hybrid form. A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw or be cursed with  (see the Monster Manual). The curse lasts until removed by the [[Remove Curse]] spell or similar magic.
