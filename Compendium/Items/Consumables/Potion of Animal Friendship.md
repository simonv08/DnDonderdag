# Potion of Animal Friendship

When you drink this potion, you can cast the [[Animal Friendship]] spell (save 13) for 1 hour at will. Agitating this muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird tongue, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair.
