# Elixir of Health

When you drink this potion, it cures any disease afflicting you, and it removes the [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]], and [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions. The clear red liquid has tiny bubbles of light in it.
