# Potion of Invisibility

This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid. When you drink it, you become [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] for 1 hour. Anything you wear or carry is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] with you. The effect ends early if you attack or cast a spell.
