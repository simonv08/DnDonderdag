# Euryale's Aegis

This gleaming brass shield bears a relief of the legendary medusa druid [[Euryale]].

While wielding this shield, you gain the following benefits:

- **Blessing of Euryale.** You have resistance to poison damage and are immune to the [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] condition.
- **Petrifying Heraldry.** As a bonus action, you can make the front of the shield flare with a medusa's petrifying magic, causing the relief's eyes to glow brightly. Choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The creature must succeed on a 20 Constitution saving throw, or it has the [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] condition as its body turns to stone. The [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] creature must make another 20 Constitution saving throw at the start of its next turn. On a failed save, the creature has the [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] condition ends. Once this bonus action is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
- **Spellcasting.** While wielding the shield, you can use an action to cast one of the following spells from it: [[Lesser Restoration]], [[Locate Creature]], [[Transport Via Plants]]. Once you use the shield to cast a spell, the shield can't cast that spell again until the next dawn.
