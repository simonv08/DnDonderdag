# Crystal Ball of Mind Reading

This [[Crystal Ball]] is about 6 inches in diameter. While touching it, you can cast the [[Scrying]] spell (save 17) with it.

You can use an action to cast the [[Detect Thoughts]] spell (save 17) while you are [[Scrying]] with the [[Crystal Ball]], targeting creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You don't need to concentrate on this [[Detect Thoughts]] to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if [[Scrying]] ends.
