# Trident of Fish Command

This trident is a magic weapon. It has 3 charges. While you carry it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast [[Dominate Beast]] (save 15) from it on a beast that has an innate swimming speed. The trident regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
