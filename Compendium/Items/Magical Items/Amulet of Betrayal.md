# Amulet of Betrayal

You can use a bonus action to move one of the following conditions from yourself to an ally within 60 feet of you: [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]], [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]], [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]]. When transferring [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]], move all your [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]] levels. If the chosen ally is immune to a transferred condition, the transfer fails.
