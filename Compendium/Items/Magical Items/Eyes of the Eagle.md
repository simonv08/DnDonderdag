# Eyes of the Eagle

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.
