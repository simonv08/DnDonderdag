# Helm of Telepathy

While wearing this helm, you can use an action to cast the [[Detect Thoughts]] spell (save 13) from it. As long as you maintain [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on the spell, you can use a bonus action to send a telepathic message to a creature you are focused on. It can reply—using a bonus action to do so—while your focus on it continues.

While focusing on a creature with [[Detect Thoughts]], you can use an action to cast the [[Suggestion]] spell (save 13) from the helm on that creature. Once used, the suggestion property can't be used again until the next dawn.
