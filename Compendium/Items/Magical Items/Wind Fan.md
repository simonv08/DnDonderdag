# Wind Fan

While holding this fan, you can use an action to cast the [[Gust Of Wind]] spell (save 13) from it. Once used, the fan shouldn't be used again until the next dawn. Each time it is used again before then, it has a cumulative 20% chance of not working and tearing into useless, nonmagical tatters.
