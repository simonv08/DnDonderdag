# Gloves of Thievery

These gloves are [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] while worn. While wearing them, you gain a +5 bonus to Dexterity ([[Skills#Sleight of Hand|Sleight Of Hand]]) checks and Dexterity checks made to pick locks.
