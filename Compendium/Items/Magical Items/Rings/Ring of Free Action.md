# Ring of Free Action

While you wear this ring, [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]] doesn't cost you extra movement. In addition, magic can neither reduce your speed nor cause you to be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] or [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]].
