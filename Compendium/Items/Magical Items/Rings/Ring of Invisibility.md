# Ring of Invisibility

While wearing this ring, you can turn [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] as an action. Anything you are wearing or carrying is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] with you. You remain [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] until the ring is removed, until you attack or cast a spell, or until you use a bonus action to become visible again.
