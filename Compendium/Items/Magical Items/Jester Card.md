# Jester Card

As an action, you can brandish this card and use it to cast the [[Otto's Irresistible Dance]] spell (save 17), and you have advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] on it. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
