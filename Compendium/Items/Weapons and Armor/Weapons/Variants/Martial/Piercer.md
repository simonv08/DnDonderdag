# Piercer

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

A character attuned to the sword regains the maximum possible number of hit points from expended Hit Dice. However, the attuned character must eat twice as much food each day (a minimum of 2 pounds) to avoid [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]] (see "The Environment" in chapter 8 of the Player's Handbook.)
