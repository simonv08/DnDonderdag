# Obsidian Flint Dragon Plate

You gain a +2 bonus to AC and resistance to poison damage while you wear this armor. In addition, you gain advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to avoid or end the [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] condition on yourself.
