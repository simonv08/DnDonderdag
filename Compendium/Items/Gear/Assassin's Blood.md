# Assassin's Blood

A creature subjected to this poison must make a 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 6 (1d12) poison damage and is [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and isn't [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]].
