# Truth Serum

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature can't knowingly speak a lie, as if under the effect of a [[Zone Of Truth]] spell.
