# Olisuba Leaf

These dried leaves of the Olisuba tree, when steeped to make a tea, can help a body recover from strenuous activity. If you drink a dose of Olisuba tea during a long rest, your [[Conditions#exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level is reduced by 2 instead of 1 at the end of that long rest.
