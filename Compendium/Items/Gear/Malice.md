# Malice

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature is [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]].
