# Ryath Root

Any creature that ingests a ryath root gains 2d4 temporary hit points. A creature that consumes more than one ryath root in a 24-hour period must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or suffer the [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
