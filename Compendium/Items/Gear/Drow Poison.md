# Drow Poison

This poison is typically made only by the [[Drow]], and only in a place far removed from sunlight. A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the creature is also [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
