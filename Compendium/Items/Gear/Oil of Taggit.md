# Oil of Taggit

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 24 hours. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature is [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]]. The creature wakes up if it takes damage.
