# Serpent Venom

This poison must be harvested from a dead or [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] [[Giant Poisonous Snake]]. A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
