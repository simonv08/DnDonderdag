# Purple Worm Poison

This poison must be harvested from a dead or [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] [[Purple Worm]]. A creature subjected to this poison must make a 19 Constitution saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
