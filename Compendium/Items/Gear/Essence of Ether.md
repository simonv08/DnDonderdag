# Essence of Ether

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 8 hours. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature is [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]]. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
