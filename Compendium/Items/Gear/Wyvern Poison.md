# Wyvern Poison

This poison must be harvested from a dead or [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] [[Wyvern]]. A creature subjected to this poison must make a 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
