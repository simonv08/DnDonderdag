# Manacles

These metal restraints can bind a Small or Medium creature. Escaping the manacles requires a successful 20 Dexterity check. Breaking them requires a successful 20 Strength check. Each set of manacles comes with one key. Without the key, a creature proficient with [[Thieves' Tools]] can pick the manacles' lock with a successful 15 Dexterity check. Manacles have 15 hit points.
