# Carrion Crawler Mucus

This poison must be harvested from a dead or [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] [[Carrion Crawler]]. A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature is [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]]. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
