# Torpor

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a 15 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 4d6 hours. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] creature is [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].
