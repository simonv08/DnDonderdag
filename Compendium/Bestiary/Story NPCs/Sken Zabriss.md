# Sken Zabriss

Medium Humanoid (Goliath), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 45
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic, Giant
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 11 | 15 | 13 | 10 | 12 |

### Traits
***Powerful Build.*** Sken counts as one size larger when determining her carrying capacity and the weight she can push, drag, or lift.

***Special Equipment.*** Sken wears a [[Ring Of Obscuring]]. With it, she can cast the [[Fog Cloud]] spell centered on herself three times per day. The cloud lasts for 1 minute (no [[Statuss#concentration|Concentration]] required).

### Actions
***Longsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage when used with two hands.

### Reactions
***Stone's Endurance (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** When Sken takes damage, she can use her reaction to reduce the damage taken by 8 (1d12 + 2).
