# Thane Kayalithica

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 170
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Athletics +12, Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Giant
**Challenge**: 7

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The giant's innate spellcasting ability is Wisdom. It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **3/day each:** [[Meld Into Stone]], [[Stone Shape]]
- **1/day each:** [[Stoneskin]], [[Time Stop]]

### Traits
***Stone Camouflage.*** The giant has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

***Olach Morrah.*** The giant meditates for 1 hour, during which time it can do nothing else. At the end of the hour, provided the giant's meditation has been uninterrupted, it becomes [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] for 8 hours. At the end of this time, the giant is no longer [[Conditions#petrified|Petrified]] and gains tremorsense out to a range of 30 feet, as well as a measure of innate spellcasting ability for the next 24 hours.

### Actions
***Fling.*** The giant tries to throw a Small or Medium creature within 10 feet of it. The target must succeed on a 17 Dexterity saving throw or be hurled up to 60 feet horizontally in a direction of the giant's choice. and land [[Conditions#prone|Prone]], taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown.

***Rolling Rock.*** The giant sends a rock tumbling along the ground in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage and falling [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] on a failed save

***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two adamantine greatclub attacks.

***Greatclub.*** 9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** 9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Reactions
***Rock Catching.*** If a rock or similar object is hurled at the giant, the giant can, with a successful 10 Dexterity saving throw, catch the missile and take no bludgeoning damage from it.
