### Traits
***Brave.*** Liara has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].

***Flaming Fury.*** Once per turn, when Liara hits a creature with a melee weapon, she can cause fire to magically erupt from her weapon and deal an extra 10 (3d6) fire damage to the target.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Liara makes three melee attacks.

***Battleaxe.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage when used with two hands.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** 3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.
