### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The giant's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 17). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Feather Fall]], [[Levitate]], [[Light]]
- **3/day each:** [[Control Weather]], [[Water Breathing]]

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** Hekaton can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Hekaton makes two broken chain attacks.

***Broken Chain.*** 14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 18 (3d6 + 9) bludgeoning damage.

***Ballista.*** 6 to hit, range 120/480 ft., one target. 18 (3d10 + 2) piercing damage.

***Lightning Strike (recharge: 5).*** Hekaton hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point he can see within 500 feet of it. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Thunderous Stomp .*** Hekaton stomps the ground, triggering a thunderclap. All other creatures within 15 feet of him must succeed on a 17 Constitution saving throw or take 33 (6d10) thunder damage and be [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]] until the start of Hekaton's next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]]. The thunderclap can be heard out to a range of 1,200 feet.
