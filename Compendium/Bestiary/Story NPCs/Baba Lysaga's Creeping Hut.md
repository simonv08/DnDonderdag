# Baba Lysaga's Creeping Hut

Gargantuan Construct, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 263
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Senses**: Blindsight 120 ft. (blind beyond this radius)
**Challenge**: 11

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 26 | 7 | 20 | 1 | 3 | 3 |

### Traits
***Constructed Nature.*** An animated object doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep. The magic that animates an object is dispelled when the construct drops to 0 hit points. An animated object reduced to 0 hit points becomes inanimate and is too damaged to be of much use or value to anyone.

***Antimagic Susceptibility.*** The hut is [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] while the magic gem that animates it is in the area of an [[Antimagic Field]]. If targeted by [[Dispel Magic]], the hut must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] for 1 minute.

***Siege Monster.*** The hut deals double damage to objects and structures.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hut makes three attacks with its roots. It can replace one of these attacks with a rock attack.

***Root.*** 12 to hit, reach 60 ft., one target. 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage.

***Rock.*** 12 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. 21 (3d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage.
