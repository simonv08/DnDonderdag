### Traits
***Chameleon Skin.*** The troglodyte has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide.

***Stench.*** Any creature other than a troglodyte that starts its turn within 5 feet of the troglodyte must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the start of the creature's next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the stench of all troglodytes for 1 hour.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the troglodyte has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The troglodyte makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.
