### Traits
***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If Harshnag fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions
***Weighted Net.*** 5 to hit, ranged 20/60 ft., one Small, Medium, or Large creature. The target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] until it escapes the net. Any creature can use its action to make a 17 Strength check to free itself or another creature in the net, ending the effect on a success. Dealing 15 slashing damage to the net (AC 12) destroys the net and frees the target.

***Multiattack.*** The giant makes two greataxe attacks.

***Gurt's Greataxe.*** 11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 26 (3d12 + 7) slashing damage, or 39 (5d12 + 7) slashing damage if the target is human.

***Rock.*** 9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
