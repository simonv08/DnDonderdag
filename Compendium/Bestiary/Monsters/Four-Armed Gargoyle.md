# Four-Armed Gargoyle

Medium Elemental, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 63
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Terran
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |

### Traits
***False Appearance.*** While the gargoyle remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate statue.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gargoyle makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
