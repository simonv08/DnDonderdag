# Aeorian Nullifier

Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 180
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +6, Survival +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Understands Draconic but can't speak
**Challenge**: 12

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 14 | 18 | 7 | 14 | 18 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The nullifier's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 16). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Counterspell]] (see "Reactions" below), [[Detect Magic]], [[Dispel Magic]], [[See Invisibility]]
- **1/day:** [[Antimagic Field]]

### Traits
***Horrid Gnashing.*** The nullifier's mouths gnash incoherently while it can see any enemies. Each creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the nullifier and can hear it must make a 16 Wisdom saving throw. Unless the save succeeds, the creature rolls a d8 to determine what it does during the current turn: 
- ***1-4:.*** The creature is [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]] until the end of the turn.
- ***5-6:.*** The creature is [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] until the end of the turn and uses its movement to get as far as possible from the nullifier.
- ***7-8:.*** The creature doesn't move, and it uses its action to make one melee attack against a random creature (other than itself) if one is within reach. It otherwise does nothing

***Magic Resistance.*** The nullifier has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The nullifier makes three attacks: one with its bites and two with its claws.

***Bites.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 17 (2d12 + 4) piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) force damage.

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) force damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 16) if it's a creature. The nullifier has two claws, each of which can grapple one creature.

### Reactions
***Counterspell.*** The nullifier attempts to interrupt a creature that it can see within 60 feet in the process of casting a spell. If the creature is casting a spell of 3rd level or lower, its spell fails and has no effect. If it is casting a spell of 4th level or higher, the nullifier makes a Charisma check with a DC equal to 10 + the spell's level. On a success, the creature's spell fails and has no effect.
