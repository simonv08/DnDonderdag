# Frost Salamander

Huge Elemental, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 168
**Speed**: walk 60 ft., burrow 40 ft., climb 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Primordial
**Challenge**: 9

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 12 | 18 | 7 | 11 | 7 |

### Traits
***Burning Fury.*** When the salamander takes fire damage, its Freezing Breath automatically recharges.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The salamander makes five attacks: four with its claws and one with its bite.

***Claws.*** 9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage.

***Bite.*** 9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. 9 (1d8 + 5) piercing damage and 5 (1d10) cold damage.

***Freezing Breath .*** The salamander exhales chill wind in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 44 (8d10) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
