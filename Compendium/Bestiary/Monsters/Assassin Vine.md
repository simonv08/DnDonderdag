# Assassin Vine

Large Plant, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 5 ft., climb 5 ft.
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft.
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 10 | 16 | 1 | 10 | 1 |

### Traits
***False Appearance.*** While the assassin vine remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal plant.

### Actions
***Constrict.*** 6 to hit, reach 20 ft., one creature. The target takes 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 14). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and it takes 21 (6d6) poison damage at the start of each of its turns. The vine can constrict only one target at a time.

***Entangling Vines.*** The assassin vine can animate normal vines and roots on the ground in a 15-foot square within 30 feet of it. These plants turn the ground in that area into [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]]. A creature in that area when the effect begins must succeed on a 13 Strength saving throw or be [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by entangling vines and roots. A creature [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] by the plants can use its action to make a 13 Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) check, freeing itself on a successful check. The effect ends after 1 minute or when the assassin vine dies or uses Entangling Vines again.
