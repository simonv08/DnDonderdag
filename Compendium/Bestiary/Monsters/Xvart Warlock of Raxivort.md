### Traits
***Low Cunning.*** The xvart can take the [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

***Overbearing Pack.*** The xvart has advantage on Strength ([[Skills#Athletics|Athletics]]) checks to shove a creature if at least one of the xvart's allies is within 5 feet of the target and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Raxivort's Tongue.*** The xvart can communicate with ordinary bats and rats, as well as giant bats and giant rats.
