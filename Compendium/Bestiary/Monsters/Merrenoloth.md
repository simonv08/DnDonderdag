### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The merrenoloth's innate spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save 13). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Charm Person]], [[Darkness]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Dispel Magic]], [[Gust Of Wind]]
- **1/day:** [[Control Weather]]
- **3/day:** [[Control Water]]

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The merrenoloth has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** The merrenoloth's weapon attacks are magical.

***Teleport.*** As a bonus action, the merrenoloth magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The merrenoloth uses Fear Gaze once and makes one oar attack.

***Oar.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage.

***Fear Gaze.*** The merrenoloth targets one creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The target must succeed on a 13 Wisdom saving throw or become [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] for 1 minute. The [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
