### Traits
***Aggressive.*** As a bonus action, the girallon can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.

***Keen Smell.*** The girallon has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The girallon makes five attacks: one with its bite and four with its claws.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 6 to hit. reach 10 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage.
