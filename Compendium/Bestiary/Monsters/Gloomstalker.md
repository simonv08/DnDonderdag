# Gloomstalker

Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 90
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., fly 80 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Dexterity
**Skills**: Athletics +9, Intimidation +5, Perception +6, Stealth +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 240 ft
**Languages**: Understands Common but can't speak
**Challenge**: 6

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22 | 16 | 14 | 5 | 17 | 14 |

### Traits
***Shadowstep.*** As a bonus action, the gloomstalker can teleport up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the gloomstalker has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gloomstalker makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

***Claws.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

***Snatch.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Medium or smaller creature. 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 17). While [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] in this way, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]].

***Shriek .*** The gloomstalker emits a terrible shriek. Each enemy within 60 feet of the gloomstalker that can hear it must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] until the end of the enemy's next turn.
