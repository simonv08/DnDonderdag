# Wyvern

Large Dragon, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 110
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., fly 80 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 6

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 10 | 16 | 5 | 12 | 6 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The wyvern makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its stinger. While flying, it can use its claws in place of one other attack.

***Bite.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage.

***Stinger.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. The target must make a 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
