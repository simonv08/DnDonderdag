# Homunculus

Tiny Construct, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 5
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
**Challenge**: 0

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 15 | 11 | 10 | 10 | 7 |

### Traits
***Telepathic Bond.*** While the homunculus is on the same plane of existence as its master, it can magically convey what it senses to its master, and the two can communicate telepathically.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 1 piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 10 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is instead [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 5 (1d10) minutes and [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way.
