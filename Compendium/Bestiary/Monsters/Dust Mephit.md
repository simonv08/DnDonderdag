# Dust Mephit

Small Elemental, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: undefined

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting (1/Day).***
The mephit can innately cast [[Sleep]], requiring no material components. Its innate spellcasting ability is Charisma.
- **At will:** [[Sleep]]

### Traits
***Death Burst.*** When the mephit dies, it explodes in a burst of dust. Each creature within 5 feet of it must then succeed on a 10 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] for 1 minute. A [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] creature can repeat the saving throw on each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Actions
***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

***Blinding Breath .*** The mephit exhales a 15-foot cone of blinding dust. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 10 Dexterity saving throw or be [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]] for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
