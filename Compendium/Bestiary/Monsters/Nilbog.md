# Nilbog

Small Humanoid (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 7
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Goblin
**Challenge**: 1

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 14 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 15 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The nilbog's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 12). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Mage Hand]], [[Tasha's Hideous Laughter]], [[Vicious Mockery]]
- **1/day:** [[Confusion]]

### Traits
***Nilbogism.*** Any creature that attempts to damage the nilbog must first succeed on a 12 Charisma saving throw or be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] until the end of the creature's next turn. A creature [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] in this way must use its action praising the nilbog. The nilbog can't regain hit points, including through magical healing, except through its Reversal of Fortune reaction.

***Nimble Escape.*** The nilbog can take the [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] or [[Actions#Hide|Hide]] action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Actions
***Fool's Scepter.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

***Shortbow.*** 4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

### Reactions
***Reversal of Fortune.*** In response to another creature dealing damage to the nilbog, the nilbog reduces the damage to 0 and regains 1d6 hit points.
