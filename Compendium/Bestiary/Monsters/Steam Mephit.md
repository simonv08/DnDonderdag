# Steam Mephit

Small Elemental, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: undefined
**Hit Points**: 21
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Aquan, Ignan
**Challenge**: 1/4

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting (1/Day).***
The mephit can innately cast [[Blur]], requiring no material components. Its innate spellcasting ability is Charisma.
- **At will:** [[Blur]]

### Traits
***Death Burst.*** When the mephit dies, it explodes in a cloud of steam. Each creature within 5 feet of the mephit must succeed on a 10 Dexterity saving throw or take 4 (1d8) fire damage.

### Actions
***Claws.*** 2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 2 (1d4) slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) fire damage.

***Steam Breath .*** The mephit exhales a 15-foot cone of scalding steam. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 10 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
