# Warforged Soldier

Medium Humanoid (Warforged), A

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 30
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +5, Perception +4, Survival +4
**Languages**: Common
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 12 | 16 | 10 | 14 | 11 |

### Traits
***Warforged Resilience.*** The warforged has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] and is immune to disease. Magic can't put it to sleep.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The warforged makes two armblade attacks.

***Armblade.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Reactions
***Protection.*** When an attacker the warforged can see makes an attack roll against a creature within 5 feet of the warforged, the warforged can impose disadvantage on the attack roll.
