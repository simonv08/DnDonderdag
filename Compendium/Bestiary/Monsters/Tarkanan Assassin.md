# Tarkanan Assassin

Medium Humanoid (Any race), Lawful NX Chaotic NY Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 45
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +3, Deception +2, Perception +4, Sleight of hand +5, Stealth +5
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Thieves' cant
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 16 | 14 | 10 | 14 | 11 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The assassin's spellcasting ability is Constitution (4 to hit with spell attacks). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Fire Bolt]]
- **1/day:** [[Chromatic Orb]]

### Traits
***Unstable Mark.*** When the assassin casts an innate spell, each creature within 10 feet of the assassin must make a 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The assassin makes two shortsword attacks.

***Shortsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

***Fire Bolt (Cantrip).*** 4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. 11 (2d10) fire damage. A flammable object hit by this spell ignites if it isn't being worn or carried.

***Chromatic Orb (1/Day).*** 4 to hit, range 90 ft., one creature. 18 (4d8) damage of a type chosen by the assassin: acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder.
