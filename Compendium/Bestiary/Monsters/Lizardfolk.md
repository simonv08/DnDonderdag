# Lizardfolk

Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk), Neutral

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 22
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +4, Survival +5
**Languages**: Draconic
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Hold Breath.*** The lizardfolk can hold its breath for 15 minutes.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The lizardfolk makes two melee attacks, each one with a different weapon.

***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Heavy Club.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

***Javelin.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Spiked Shield.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
