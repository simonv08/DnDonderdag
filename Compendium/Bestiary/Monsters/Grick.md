# Grick

Medium Monstrosity, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 27
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 14 | 11 | 3 | 14 | 5 |

### Traits
***Stone Camouflage.*** The grick has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The grick makes one attack with its tentacles. If that attack hits, the grick can make one beak attack against the same target.

***Tentacles.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (2d6 + 2) slashing damage.

***Beak.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
