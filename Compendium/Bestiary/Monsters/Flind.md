# Flind

Medium Humanoid (Gnoll), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 127
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Intimidation +5, Perception +5
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Gnoll
**Challenge**: 9

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 10 | 19 | 11 | 13 | 12 |

### Traits
***Aura of Blood Thirst.*** If the flind isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]], any creature with the Rampage trait can make a bite attack as a bonus action while within 10 feet of the flind.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The flind makes three attacks: one with each of its different flail attacks or three with its longbow.

***Flail of Madness.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target must make a 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target must make a melee attack against a random target within its reach on its next turn. If it has no targets within its reach even after moving, it loses its action on that turn.

***Flail of Pain.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 22 (4d10) psychic damage.

***Flail of Paralysis.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a 16 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] until the end of its next turn.

***Longbow.*** 4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 4 (1d8) piercing damage.
