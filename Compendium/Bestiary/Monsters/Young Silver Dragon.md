### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage.

***Breath Weapons (recharge: 5).*** The dragon uses one of the following breath weapons. 
- ***Cold Breath.*** The dragon exhales an icy blast in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
- ***Paralyzing Breath.*** The dragon exhales paralyzing gas in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 17 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
