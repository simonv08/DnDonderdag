# Female Steeder

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 30
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +7, Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 16 | 14 | 2 | 10 | 3 |

### Traits
***Spider Climb.*** The steeder can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Extraordinary Leap.*** The distance of the steeder's long jumps is tripled; every foot of its walking speed that it spends on the jump allows it to move 3 feet.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) poison damage.

***Sticky Leg.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Medium or smaller creature. The target is stuck to the steeder's leg and is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] until it escapes (escape 12). The steeder can have only one creature [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] at a time.
