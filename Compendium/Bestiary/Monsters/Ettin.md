# Ettin

Large Giant, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 12
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Giant, Orc
**Challenge**: 4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | 8 | 17 | 6 | 10 | 8 |

### Traits
***Two Heads.*** The ettin has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks and on saving throws against being [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]], and knocked [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]].

***Wakeful.*** When one of the ettin's heads is asleep, its other head is awake.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The ettin makes two attacks: one with its battleaxe and one with its morningstar.

***Battleaxe.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.

***Morningstar.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage.
