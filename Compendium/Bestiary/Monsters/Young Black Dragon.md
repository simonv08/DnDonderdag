# Young Black Dragon

Large Dragon, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 127
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +6, Stealth +5
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 14 | 17 | 12 | 11 | 15 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The dragon can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) acid damage.

***Claw.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Acid Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales acid in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 49 (11d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
