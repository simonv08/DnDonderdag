# Core Spawn Emissary

Medium Aberration, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 102
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Languages**: Telepathy 120 ft., Understands Deep Speech but can't speak
**Challenge**: 6

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 15 | 18 | 8 | 13 | 8 |

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The emissary has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The emissary makes three talons attacks.

***Talons.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 14 (2d10 + 3) slashing damage.

***Alluring Thrum (recharge: 5).*** The emissary emits a dreadful yet alluring hum. Each creature within 20 feet of the emissary that can hear it and that isn't an aberration must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Crystal Spores .*** A 15-foot-radius cloud of toxic crystalline spores extends out from the emissary. The spores spread around corners. Each creature in the area must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]]. While [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way, a creature takes 11 (2d10) poison damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
