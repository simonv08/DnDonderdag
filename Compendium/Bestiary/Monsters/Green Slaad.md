# Green Slaad

Large Aberration (Shapechanger), Chaotic Neutral

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 127
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Arcana +3, Perception +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Slaad, Telepathy 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 8

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 15 | 16 | 11 | 8 | 12 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The slaad's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 12). The slaad can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Detect Thoughts]], [[Mage Hand]]
- **1/day:** [[Fireball]]
- **2/day each:** [[Fear]], [[Invisibility]] (self only)

### Traits
***Shapechanger.*** The slaad can use its action to polymorph into a Small or Medium humanoid, or back into its true form. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.

***Magic Resistance.*** The slaad has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Regeneration.*** The slaad regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The slaad makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws or staff. Alternatively, it uses its Hurl Flame twice.

***Bite (Slaad Form Only).*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.

***Claw (Slaad Form Only).*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Staff.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Hurl Flame.*** 4 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. 10 (3d6) fire damage. The fire ignites flammable objects that aren't being worn or carried.
