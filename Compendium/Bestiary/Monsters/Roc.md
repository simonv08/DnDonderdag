# Roc

Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 248
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., fly 120 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Challenge**: 11

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 28 | 10 | 20 | 3 | 10 | 9 |

### Traits
***Keen Sight.*** The roc has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The roc makes two attacks: one with its beak and one with its talons.

***Beak.*** 13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 27 (4d8 + 9) piercing damage.

***Talons.*** 13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 23 (4d6 + 9) slashing damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 19). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the roc can't use its talons on another target.
