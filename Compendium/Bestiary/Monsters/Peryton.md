# Peryton

Medium Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 33
**Speed**: walk 20 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +5
**Languages**: Understands Common and Elvish but can't speak
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 12 | 13 | 9 | 12 | 10 |

### Traits
***Dive Attack.*** If the peryton is flying and dives at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee weapon attack, the attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) damage to the target.

***Flyby.*** The peryton doesn't provoke an opportunity attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

***Keen Sight and Smell.*** The peryton has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight or smell.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The peryton makes one gore attack and one talon attack.

***Gore.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.

***Talons.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (2d4 + 3) piercing damage.
