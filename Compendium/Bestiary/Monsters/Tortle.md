# Tortle

Medium Humanoid (Tortle), Lawful Good

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 22
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +4, Survival +3
**Languages**: Aquan, Common
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 10 | 12 | 11 | 13 | 12 |

### Traits
***Hold Breath.*** The tortle can hold its breath for 1 hour.

### Actions
***Claws.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

***Quarterstaff.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage if used with two hands.

***Light Crossbow.*** 2 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. 4 (1d8) piercing damage.

***Shell Defense.*** The tortle withdraws into its shell. Until it emerges, it gains a +4 bonus to AC and has advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws. While in its shell, the tortle is [[Conditions#prone|Prone]], its speed is 0 and can't increase, it has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws, it can't take reactions, and the only action it can take is a bonus action to emerge.
