### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The drow's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 11). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Dancing Lights]]
- **1/day each:** [[Darkness]], [[Faerie Fire]]

### Traits
***Fey Ancestry.*** The drow has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't put the drow to sleep.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the drow has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Shortsword.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

***Hand Crossbow.*** 4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The target wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
