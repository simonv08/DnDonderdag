# Bone Devil

Large Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 142
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., fly 40 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills**: Deception +7, Insight +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Infernal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 9

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 16 | 18 | 13 | 14 | 16 |

### Traits
***Devil's Sight.*** Magical darkness doesn't impede the devil's darkvision.

***Magic Resistance.*** The devil has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The devil makes three attacks: two with its claws and one with its sting.

***Claw.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.

***Sting.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
