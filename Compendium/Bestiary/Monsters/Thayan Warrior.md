### Traits
***Doomvault Devotion.*** Within the Doomvault, the warrior has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] or [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].

***Pack Tactics.*** The warrior has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the warrior's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The warrior makes two melee attacks.

***Longsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

***Javelin.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
