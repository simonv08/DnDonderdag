# Goblin Boss

Small Humanoid (Goblinoid), Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 21
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Goblin
**Challenge**: 1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 14 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 10 |

### Traits
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin can take the [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] or [[Actions#Hide|Hide]] action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The goblin makes two attacks with its scimitar. The second attack has disadvantage.

***Scimitar.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** 2 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. 3 (1d6) piercing damage.

### Reactions
***Redirect Attack.*** When a creature the goblin can see targets it with an attack, the goblin chooses another goblin within 5 feet of it. The two goblins swap places, and the chosen goblin becomes the target instead.
