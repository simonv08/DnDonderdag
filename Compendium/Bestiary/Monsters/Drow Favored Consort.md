### Traits
***Fey Ancestry.*** The drow has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't put the drow to sleep.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the drow has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

***War Magic.*** When the drow uses its action to cast a spell, it can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The drow makes three scimitar attacks.

***Scimitar.*** 11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 18 (4d8) poison damage. In addition, the target has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against a spell the drow casts before the end of the drow's next turn.

***Hand Crossbow.*** 11 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The target regains consciousness if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it.
