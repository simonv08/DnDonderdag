# Sea Lion

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 90
**Speed**: walk 10 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +5
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 15 | 15 | 3 | 12 | 8 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The sea lion can breathe air and water.

***Keen Smell.*** The sea lion has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The sea lion has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the sea lion's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Swimming Leap.*** With a 10-foot swimming start, the sea lion can long jump out of or across the water up to 25 feet.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The sea lion makes three attacks: one bite attack and two claw attacks.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.
