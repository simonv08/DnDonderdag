### Traits
***Commander of Bones.*** As a bonus action, the knight can target one skeleton or zombie it can see within 30 feet of it. The target must make a 14 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target must obey the knight's commands until the knight dies or until the knight releases it as a bonus action. The knight can command up to twelve undead at a time this way.

***Master of the Pallid Banner.*** While within 60 feet of the knight, any undead ally of the knight has advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The knight attacks twice with one of its weapons.

***Greatsword.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Longbow.*** 4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
