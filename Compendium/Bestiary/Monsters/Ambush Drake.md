# Ambush Drake

Medium Dragon, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 22
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +4
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Understands Draconic but can't speak it
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | 15 | 14 | 4 | 11 | 6 |

### Traits
***Pack Tactics.*** The drake has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the drake's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Surprise Attack.*** If the drake surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage from the attack.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.
