# Pit Fiend

Large Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 300
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.
**Languages**: Infernal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 20

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 26 | 14 | 24 | 22 | 18 | 24 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The pit fiend's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 21). The pit fiend can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Fireball]]
- **3/day each:** [[Hold Monster]], [[Wall Of Fire]]

### Traits
***Fear Aura.*** Any creature hostile to the pit fiend that starts its turn within 20 feet of the pit fiend must make a 21 Wisdom saving throw, unless the pit fiend is [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]]. On a failed save, the creature is [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]] until the start of its next turn. If a creature's saving throw is successful, the creature is immune to the pit fiend's Fear Aura for the next 24 hours.

***Magic Resistance.*** The pit fiend has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** The pit fiend's weapon attacks are magical.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The pit fiend makes four attacks: one with its bite, one with its claw, one with its mace, and one with its tail.

***Bite.*** 14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 22 (4d6 + 8) piercing damage. The target must succeed on a 21 Constitution saving throw or become [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]]. While [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way, the target can't regain hit points, and it takes 21 (6d6) poison damage at the start of each of its turns. The [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Claw.*** 14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 17 (2d8 + 8) slashing damage.

***Mace.*** 14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 15 (2d6 + 8) bludgeoning damage plus 21 (6d6) fire damage.

***Tail.*** 14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 24 (3d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage.
