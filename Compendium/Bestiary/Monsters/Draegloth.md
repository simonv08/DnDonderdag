# Draegloth

Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 15
**Hit Points**: 123
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +5
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Elvish, Undercommon
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 15 | 18 | 13 | 11 | 11 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The draegloth's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 11). The draegloth can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Darkness]]
- **1/day each:** [[Confusion]], [[Dancing Lights]], [[Faerie Fire]]

### Traits
***Fey Ancestry.*** The draegloth has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't put it to sleep.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The draegloth makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 16 (2d10 + 5) slashing damage.
