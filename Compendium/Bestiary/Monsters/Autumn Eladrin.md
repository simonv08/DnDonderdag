# Autumn Eladrin

Medium Fey (Elf), Chaotic Neutral

**Armor Class**: 19
**Hit Points**: 127
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Insight +7, Medicine +7
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Elvish, Sylvan
**Challenge**: 10

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The eladrin's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 16). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Calm Emotions]], [[Sleep]]
- **3/day each:** [[Cure Wounds]] (as a 5th-level spell), [[Lesser Restoration]]
- **1/day each:** [[Greater Restoration]], [[Heal]], [[Raise Dead]]

### Traits
***Enchanting Presence.*** Any non-eladrin creature that starts its turn within 60 feet of the eladrin must make a 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] by the eladrin for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature becomes immune to any eladrin's Enchanting Presence for 24 hours. Whenever the eladrin deals damage to the [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] creature, the creature can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Fey Step (recharge: 4).*** As a bonus action, the eladrin can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

***Magic Resistance.*** The eladrin has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Longsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage plus 18 (4d8) psychic damage, or 6 (1d10 + 1) slashing damage plus 18 (4d8) psychic damage if used with two hands.

***Longbow.*** 7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) psychic damage.

### Reactions
***Foster Peace.*** If a creature [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] by the eladrin hits with an attack roll while within 60 feet of the eladrin, the eladrin magically causes the attack to miss, provided the eladrin can see the attacker.
