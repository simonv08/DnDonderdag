# Pentadrone

Large Construct, Lawful Neutral

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 32
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Senses**: Truesight 120 ft.
**Languages**: Modron
**Challenge**: 2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | 14 | 12 | 10 | 10 | 13 |

### Traits
***Axiomatic Mind.*** The pentadrone can't be compelled to act in a manner contrary to its nature or its instructions.

***Disintegration.*** If the pentadrone dies, its body disintegrates into dust, leaving behind its weapons and anything else it was carrying.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The pentadrone makes five arm attacks.

***Arm.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

***Paralysis Gas (recharge: 5).*** The pentadrone exhales a 30-foot cone of gas. Each creature in that area must succeed on a 11 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
