# Bulette

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 94
**Speed**: walk 40 ft., burrow 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +6
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 11 | 21 | 2 | 10 | 5 |

### Traits
***Standing Leap.*** The bulette's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 30 (4d12 + 4) piercing damage.

***Deadly Leap.*** If the bulette jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a 16 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] and take 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (3d6 + 4) slashing damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]], and is pushed 5 feet out of the bulette's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] in the bulette's space.
