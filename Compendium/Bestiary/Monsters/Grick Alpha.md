# Grick Alpha

Large Monstrosity, Neutral

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 75
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 7

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 16 | 15 | 4 | 14 | 9 |

### Traits
***Stone Camouflage.*** The grick has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The grick makes two attacks: one with its tail and one with its tentacles. If it hits with its tentacles, the grick can make one beak attack against the same target.

***Tail.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Tentacles.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 22 (4d8 + 4) slashing damage.

***Beak.*** 7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.
