### Traits
***Invisible in Water.*** The water weird is [[Conditions#invisible|Invisible]] while fully immersed in water.

***Water Bound.*** The water weird dies if it leaves the water to which it is bound or if that water is destroyed.

### Actions
***Constrict.*** 5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. 13 (3d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 13) and pulled 5 feet toward the water weird. Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], the water weird tries to drown it, and the water weird can't constrict another target.
