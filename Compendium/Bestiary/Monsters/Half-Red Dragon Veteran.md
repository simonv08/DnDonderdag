# Half-Red Dragon Veteran

Medium Humanoid (Human), A

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 65
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +6, Perception +3
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Draconic
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 13 | 14 | 10 | 11 | 10 |

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The veteran makes two longsword attacks. If it has a shortsword drawn, it can also make a shortsword attack.

***Longsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

***Shortsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** 3 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.

***Fire Breath (recharge: 5).*** The veteran exhales fire in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
