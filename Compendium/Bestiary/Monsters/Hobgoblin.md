# Hobgoblin

Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 11
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Goblin
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | 12 | 12 | 10 | 10 | 9 |

### Traits
***Martial Advantage.*** Once per turn, the hobgoblin can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 feet of an ally of the hobgoblin that isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Longsword.*** 3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage, or 6 (1d10 + 1) slashing damage if used with two hands.

***Longbow.*** 3 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.
