# The Hungry

Medium Monstrosity, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 225
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common
**Challenge**: 11

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 10 | 17 | 6 | 11 | 6 |

### Traits
***Life Hunger.*** If a creature the Hungry can see regains hit points, the Hungry gains two benefits until the end of its next turn: it has advantage on attack rolls, and its bite deals an extra 22 (4d10) necrotic damage on a hit.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Hungry makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) necrotic damage.

***Claws.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 18 (4d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 16) and is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]] until the grapple ends. While grappling a creature, the Hungry can't attack with its claws.
