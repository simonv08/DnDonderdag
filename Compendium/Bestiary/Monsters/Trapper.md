# Trapper

Large Monstrosity, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 10 ft., climb 10 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 30 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 10 | 17 | 2 | 13 | 4 |

### Traits
***False Appearance.*** While the trapper is attached to a ceiling, floor, or wall and remains motionless, it is almost indistinguishable from an ordinary section of ceiling, floor, or wall. A creature that can see it and succeeds on a 20 Intelligence ([[Skills#Investigation|Investigation]]) or Intelligence ([[Skills#Nature|Nature]]) check can discern its presence.

***Spider Climb.*** The trapper can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Actions
***Smother.*** One Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of the trapper must succeed on a 14 Dexterity saving throw or be [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 14). Until the grapple ends, the target takes 17 (4d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage at the start of each of its turns. While [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] in this way, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], and at risk of suffocating. The trapper can smother only one creature at a time.
