# Air Elemental

Large Elemental, Neutral

**Armor Class**: undefined
**Hit Points**: 90
**Speed**: fly 90 ft. (hover)
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Auran
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | 20 | 14 | 6 | 10 | 6 |

### Traits
***Air Form.*** The elemental can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two slam attacks.

***Slam.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

***Whirlwind (recharge: 4).*** Each creature in the elemental's space must make a 13 Strength saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 15 (3d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage and is flung up 20 feet away from the elemental in a random direction and knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. If a thrown target strikes an object, such as a wall or floor, the target takes 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown. If the target is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a 13 Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. If the saving throw is successful, the target takes half the bludgeoning damage and isn't flung away or knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
