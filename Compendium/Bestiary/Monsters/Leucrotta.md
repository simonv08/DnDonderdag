# Leucrotta

Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 67
**Speed**: walk 50 ft.
**Skills**: Deception +2, Perception +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Gnoll
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 14 | 15 | 9 | 12 | 6 |

### Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The leucrotta has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

***Kicking Retreat.*** If the leucrotta attacks with its hooves, it can take the [[Actions#Disengage|Disengage]] action as a bonus action.

***Mimicry.*** The leucrotta can mimic animal sounds and humanoid voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful 14 Wisdom ([[Skills#Insight|Insight]]) check.

***Rampage.*** When the leucrotta reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, it can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make an attack with its hooves.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The leucrotta makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its hooves.

***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage. If the leucrotta scores a critical hit, it rolls the damage dice three times, instead of twice.

***Hooves.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
