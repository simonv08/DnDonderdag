# Karrnathi Undead Soldier

Medium Undead, Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 52
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Skills**: Athletics +5, Perception +3
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 14 | 16 | 12 | 13 | 5 |

### Traits
***Pack Tactics.*** The soldier has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the soldier's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the soldier to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the soldier drops to 1 hit point instead.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The soldier attacks three times with one of its weapons.

***Longsword.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage if used with two hands.

***Longbow.*** 4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The soldier adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the soldier must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
