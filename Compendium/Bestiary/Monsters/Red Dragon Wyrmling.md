**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +2
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.
**Languages**: Draconic
**Challenge**: 4

### Actions
***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) fire damage.

***Fire Breath (recharge: 5).*** The dragon exhales fire in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
