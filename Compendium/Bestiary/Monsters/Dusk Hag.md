# Dusk Hag

Medium Fey, Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 82
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Intelligence, Wisdom
**Skills**: Deception +7, Insight +6, Perception +6
**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft.
**Languages**: Common, Giant, Infernal
**Challenge**: 6

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | 14 | 12 | 17 | 16 | 18 |

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The hag's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 15). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Disguise Self]]
- **3/day each:** [[Dream]], [[Hypnotic Pattern]], [[Sleep]] (9d8)
- **1/day each:** [[Legend Lore]], [[Scrying]]

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The hag has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hag makes two Nightmare Touch attacks.

***Claws.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

***Nightmare Touch.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 18 (4d6 + 4) psychic damage. If the target is [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]], it takes an extra 10 (3d6) psychic damage and is cursed until the hag dies or the curse is removed. The cursed creature's hit point maximum decreases by 5 (1d10) whenever it finishes a long rest.

### Reactions
***Dream Eater.*** When an [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] creature the hag can see within 30 feet of her regains consciousness, the hag can force the creature to make a 15 Wisdom saving throw. Unless the save succeeds, the creature takes 11 (2d10) psychic damage, and the hag regains hit points equal to the amount of damage taken.
