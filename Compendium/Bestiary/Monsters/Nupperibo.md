# Nupperibo

Medium Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 11
**Speed**: walk 20 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +1
**Senses**: Blindsight 10 ft. (blind beyond this radius)
**Languages**: Understands Infernal but can't speak
**Challenge**: 1/2

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Traits
***Cloud of Vermin.*** Any creature, other than a devil, that starts its turn within 20 feet of the nupperibo must make a 11 Constitution saving throw. A creature within the areas of two or more nupperibos makes the saving throw with disadvantage. On a failure, the creature takes 2 (1d4) piercing damage.

***Hunger-Driven.*** In the Nine Hells, the nupperibos can flawlessly track any creature that has taken damage from any nupperibo's Cloud of Vermin within the previous 24 hours.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
