# Clay Gladiator

Medium Construct, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 112
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution
**Skills**: Athletics +10, Intimidation +5
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | 15 | 16 | 10 | 12 | 15 |

### Traits
***Climbing.*** The gladiator can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Steadfast.*** The gladiator can't be disarmed, and cannot make ranged attacks.

***Brute.*** A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the gladiator hits with it (included in the attack).

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The gladiator makes three melee attacks.

***Spear.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage, or 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage if used with two hands.

***Shield Bash.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 9 (2d4 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The gladiator adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the gladiator must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
