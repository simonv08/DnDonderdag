### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The gnome's innate spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save 11). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Nondetection]] (self only)
- **1/day each:** [[Blindness/deafness]], [[Blur]], [[Disguise Self]]

### Traits
***Stone Camouflage.*** The gnome has advantage on Dexterity ([[Skills#Stealth|Stealth]]) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.

***Gnome Cunning.*** The gnome has advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.

### Actions
***War Pick.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

***Poisoned Dart.*** 4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one creature. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 minute.
