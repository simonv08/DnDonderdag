# Drow Elite Warrior

Medium Humanoid ([object Object]), Neutral Evil

**Armor Class**: 18
**Hit Points**: 71
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Skills**: Perception +4, Stealth +10
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Elvish, Undercommon
**Challenge**: 5

### Spellcasting
***Innate Spellcasting.***
The drow's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save 12). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- **At will:** [[Dancing Lights]]
- **1/day each:** [[Darkness]], [[Faerie Fire]], [[Levitate]] (self only)

### Traits
***Fey Ancestry.*** The drow has advantage on saving throws against being [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], and magic can't put the drow to sleep.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the drow has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The drow makes two shortsword attacks.

***Shortsword.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) poison damage.

***Hand Crossbow.*** 7 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a 13 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also [[Conditions#unconscious|Unconscious]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way. The target wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.

### Reactions
***Parry.*** The drow adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the drow must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
