# Hezrou

Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil

**Armor Class**: 16
**Hit Points**: 136
**Speed**: walk 30 ft.
**Saving Throws**: Strength, Constitution, Wisdom
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft.
**Languages**: Abyssal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge**: 8

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 17 | 20 | 5 | 12 | 13 |

### Traits
***Magic Resistance.*** The hezrou has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Stench.*** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the hezrou must succeed on a 14 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the start of its next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the hezrou's stench for 24 hours.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hezrou makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage.

***Claws.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
