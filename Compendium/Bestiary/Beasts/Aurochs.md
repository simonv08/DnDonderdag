### Traits
***Charge.*** If the aurochs moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a gore attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Gore.*** 7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage.
