### Traits
***Charge.*** If the elk moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Ram.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

***Hooves.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] creature. 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
