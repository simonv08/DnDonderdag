# Giant Elk

Huge Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 42
**Speed**: walk 60 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +4
**Languages**: Giant Elk, Understands Common, Elvish, Sylvan but can't speak them
**Challenge**: 2

### Traits
***Charge.*** If the elk moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Ram.*** 6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Hooves.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] creature. 22 (4d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
