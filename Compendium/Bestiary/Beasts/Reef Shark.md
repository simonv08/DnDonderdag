### Traits
***Pack Tactics.*** The shark has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the shark's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

***Water Breathing.*** The shark can breathe only underwater.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
