# Stench Kow

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: undefined

### Traits
***Charge.*** If the kow moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a gore attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage.

***Stench.*** Any creature other than a stench kow that starts its turn within 5 feet of the stench kow must succeed on a 12 Constitution saving throw or be [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the start of the creature's next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the stench of all stench kows for 1 hour.

### Actions
***Gore.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
