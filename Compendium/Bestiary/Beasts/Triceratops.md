# Triceratops

Huge Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 95
**Speed**: walk 50 ft.
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22 | 9 | 17 | 2 | 11 | 5 |

### Traits
***Trampling Charge.*** If the triceratops moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a gore attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. If the target is [[Conditions#prone|Prone]], the triceratops can make one stomp attack against it as a bonus action.

### Actions
***Gore.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 24 (4d8 + 6) piercing damage.

***Stomp.*** 9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] creature. 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
