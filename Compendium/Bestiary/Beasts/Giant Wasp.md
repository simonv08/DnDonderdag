### Actions
***Sting.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must make a 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] while [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] in this way.
