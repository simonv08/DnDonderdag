# Giant Snapping Turtle

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 17
**Hit Points**: 75
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 10 | 14 | 2 | 12 | 5 |

### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The turtle can breathe air and water.

***Stable.*** Whenever an effect knocks the turtle [[Conditions#prone|Prone]], it can make a 10 Constitution saving throw to avoid being knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. A [[Conditions#prone|Prone]] turtle is upside down. To stand up, it must succeed on a 10 Dexterity check on its turn and then use all its movement for that turn.

### Actions
***Bite.*** 6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 18 (4d6 + 4) slashing damage.
