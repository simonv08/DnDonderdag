# Giant Crocodile

Huge Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 14
**Hit Points**: 85
**Speed**: walk 30 ft., swim 50 ft.
**Skills**: Stealth +5
**Challenge**: 5

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | 9 | 17 | 2 | 10 | 7 |

### Traits
***Hold Breath.*** The crocodile can hold its breath for 30 minutes.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The crocodile makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.

***Bite.*** 8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 21 (3d10 + 5) piercing damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]], and the crocodile can't bite another target.

***Tail.*** 8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target not [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] by the crocodile. 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
