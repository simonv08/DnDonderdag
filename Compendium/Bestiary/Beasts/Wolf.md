# Wolf

Medium Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 13
**Hit Points**: 11
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Skills**: Perception +3, Stealth +4
**Challenge**: 1/4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 15 | 12 | 3 | 12 | 6 |

### Traits
***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The wolf has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Bite.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
