# Giant Lightning Eel

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: undefined
**Hit Points**: 42
**Speed**: walk 5 ft., swim 30 ft.
**Senses**: Blindsight 60 ft.
**Challenge**: 3

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | 17 | 16 | 2 | 12 | 3 |

### Traits
***Water Breathing.*** The eel can breathe only underwater.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The eel makes two bite attacks.

***Bite.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) lightning damage.

***Lightning Jolt (recharge: 5).*** One creature the eel touches within 5 feet of it outside water, or each creature within 15 feet of it in a body of water, must make a 12 Constitution saving throw. On failed save, a target takes 13 (3d8) lightning damage. If the target takes any of this damage, the target is [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]] until the end of the eel's next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage and isn't [[Conditions#stunned|Stunned]].
