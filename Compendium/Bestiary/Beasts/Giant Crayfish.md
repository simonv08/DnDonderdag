### Traits
***Amphibious.*** The giant crayfish can breathe air and water.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The giant crayfish makes two claw attacks.

***Claw.*** 4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d10 + 2) bludgeoning damage, and the target is [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]] (escape 12). The crayfish has two claws, each of which can grapple only one target.
