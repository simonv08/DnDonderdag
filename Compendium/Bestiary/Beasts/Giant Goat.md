# Giant Goat

Large Beast, Unaligned

**Armor Class**: 11
**Hit Points**: 19
**Speed**: walk 40 ft.
**Challenge**: 1/2

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | 11 | 12 | 3 | 12 | 6 |

### Traits
***Charge.*** If the goat moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 5 (2d4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

***Sure-Footed.*** The goat has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock it [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].

### Actions
***Ram.*** 5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.
