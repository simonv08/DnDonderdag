### Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The rat has advantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The rat has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the rat's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]].
