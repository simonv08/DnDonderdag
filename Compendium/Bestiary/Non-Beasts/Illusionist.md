### Traits
***Displacement (Recharges after the Illusionist Casts an Illusion Spell of 1st Level or Higher).*** As a bonus action, the illusionist projects an illusion that makes the illusionist appear to be standing in a place a few inches from its actual location, causing any creature to have disadvantage on attack rolls against the illusionist.
