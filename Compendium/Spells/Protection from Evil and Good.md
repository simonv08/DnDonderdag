# Protection from Evil and Good
### *1st-level Abjuration Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (holy water or powdered silver and iron, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** 10 minute (concentration)

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is protected against certain types of creatures: aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead.

The protection grants several benefits. Creatures of those types have disadvantage on attack rolls against the target. The target also can't be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], or possessed by them. If the target is already [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]], [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]], or possessed by such a creature, the target has advantage on any new saving throw against the relevant effect.
