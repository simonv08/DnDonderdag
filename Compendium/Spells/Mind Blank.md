# Mind Blank
### *8th-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 24 hour

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is immune to psychic damage, any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, divination spells, and the [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] condition. The spell even foils [[Wish]] spells and spells or effects of similar power used to affect the target's mind or to gain information about the target.
