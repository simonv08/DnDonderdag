# Freedom of Movement
### *4th-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap, bound around the arm or a similar appendage)
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[Rules#Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target's speed nor cause the target to be [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]] or [[Conditions#restrained|Restrained]].

The target can also spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as manacles or a creature that has it [[Conditions#grappled|Grappled]]. Finally, being underwater imposes no penalties on the target's movement or attacks.
