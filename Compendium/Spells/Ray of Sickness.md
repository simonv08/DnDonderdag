# Ray of Sickness
### *1st-level Necromancy Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A ray of sickening greenish energy lashes out toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 poison damage and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is also [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]] until the end of your next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
