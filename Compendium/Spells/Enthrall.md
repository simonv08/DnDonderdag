# Enthrall
### *2nd-level Enchantment Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Saving Throw:** Wisdom
**Duration:** 1 minute

You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range and that can hear you to make a Wisdom saving throw. Any creature that can't be [[Conditions#charmed|Charmed]] succeeds on this saving throw automatically, and if you or your companions are fighting a creature, it has advantage on the save. On a failed save, the target has disadvantage on Wisdom ([[Skills#Perception|Perception]]) checks made to perceive any creature other than you until the spell ends or until the target can no longer hear you. The spell ends if you are [[Conditions#incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or can no longer speak.
