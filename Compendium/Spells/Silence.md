# Silence
### *2nd-level Illusion Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 10 minute (concentration)

For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the sphere is immune to thunder damage, and creatures are [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]] while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a verbal component is impossible there.
