# Lesser Restoration
### *2nd-level Abjuration Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and can end either one disease or one condition afflicting it. The condition can be [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], [[Conditions#paralyzed|Paralyzed]], or [[Conditions#poisoned|Poisoned]].
