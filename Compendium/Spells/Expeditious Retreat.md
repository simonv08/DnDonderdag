# Expeditious Retreat
### *1st-level Transmutation Spell* *(Concentration)*
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 10 minute (concentration)

This spell allows you to move at an incredible pace. When you cast this spell, and then as a bonus action on each of your turns until the spell ends, you can take the [[Actions#Dash|Dash]] action.
