# Destructive Wave
### *5th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** Varies
**Components:** V
**Saving Throw:** Constitution
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You strike the ground, creating a burst of divine energy that ripples outward from you. Each creature you choose within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 5d6 thunder damage, as well as 5d6 radiant or necrotic damage (your choice), and be knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]]. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw takes half as much damage and isn't knocked [[Conditions#prone|Prone]].
