# Heal
### *6th-level Evocation Spell*
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a creature that you can see within range. A surge of positive energy washes through the creature, causing it to regain 70 hit points. This spell also ends [[Conditions#blinded|Blinded]], [[Conditions#deafened|Deafened]], and any diseases affecting the target. This spell has no effect on constructs or undead.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the amount of healing increases by 10 for each slot level above 6th.
