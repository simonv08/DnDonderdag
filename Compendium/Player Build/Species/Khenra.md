# Khenra
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 35 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** STR +1, DEX +2
**Languages:** Common, Other
## Traits
##### Alignment
Most khenra lean toward chaotic alignments. They have no particular inclination toward good or evil.
##### Khenra Weapon Training
You have proficiency with the khopesh ([[Longsword]]), [[Spear]], and [[Javelin]].
##### Khenra Twins
If your twin is alive and you can see your twin, whenever you roll a 1 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll. If your twin is dead (or if you were born without a twin), you can't be [[Conditions#frightened|Frightened]].
