# Kenku
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** DEX +2
**Languages:** Common, Auran
## Traits
##### Ambusher
In the first round of a combat, you have advantage on attack rolls against any creature who is [[Statuss#surprised|Surprised]].
##### Mimicry
You can mimic any sounds you have heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful 14 Wisdom ([[Skills#Insight|Insight]]) check.
