# Siren
## Stats
**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 25 ft. walking
**Ability Scores:** CHA +2
**Languages:** Common, Other
## Traits
##### Alignment
Most sirens lean toward chaotic alignment, cherishing the freedom and independence that comes from joining a pirate crew.
##### Siren's Song
You know the [[Friends]] cantrip and can cast it without material components.
##### Flight
You have a flying speed of 30 feet. You can't use your flying speed while you wear medium or heavy armor. (If your campaign uses the variant rule for , you can't use your flying speed if you are encumbered.)
